# Twenty-seventh sourced expansion: Cyberzoo experiments and an ETH archive

Reviewed 30 October 2026. Two additions bring the catalogue to 331 research records across 40 countries, leaving 669 toward 1,000. All prior 329 records remain unchanged.

## Identity and timing decisions

- Cyberzoo: MAVLab's 13 May 2026 Bee-Nav report explicitly identifies indoor flights at TU Delft's Cyberzoo. The earlier 27 May 2019 racing report names the same environment. These are two research uses of one facility. The 2026 page also describes outdoor trials at Unmanned Valley, already recorded separately, and unnamed indoor environments; neither creates a new record. The record is a source-reported trial, not a claim of a public delivery or landing service. The later page-update date is not substituted for its publication date.
- Flying Machine Arena: ETH's project archive explicitly identifies an indoor research space and states that archived projects are no longer updated. Its archived iterative-learning page supplies research context. Historical status refers to this archived activity; no closure date or continued operation is inferred. The old external arena domain currently displays unrelated commercial drone content. It is excluded from record source links, and the university-hosted archive is the principal source. The archive's footer contact address is not converted into a room or building coordinate.
- Both map points are broad editorial city references. Neither is a source-derived facility position. The undated ETH archive retains a null publication date; dates of referenced papers are not substituted.

## Deferred sources

Imperial pages returned access errors. The guessed NTU and Southampton facility paths did not resolve. Those names do not become records without reviewed facility evidence. The repurposed external arena domain is not treated as current ETH evidence.
