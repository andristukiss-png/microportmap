# Three community dispatch origins in Japan

Reviewed 4 November 2026. Each record describes historical activity at an approximate locality.

- [Makurase community supply trial](https://aeronext.co.jp/news/tsuwanotown-poc/): The trial used one temporary origin. Neither the receiving park nor the community hall is counted as a launch site.
- [Kokuzo Satoyama Park delivery trial](https://aeronext.co.jp/news/nomi_poc/): Only the park dispatched the aircraft. The restaurant supplied the meal and the university received it.
- [Hikariura Tunnel earthquake relief dispatch](https://f.irbank.net/pr/20240208/140120240208529330.pdf): The report names a separate origin from Wajima City Cultural Hall. The locality marker does not identify a particular tunnel portal.
