# Medical drone origins and return sites

Reviewed 2 November 2026. Six distinct records, including the first from DR Congo. Hospital pickup evidence alone is not evidence of a permanent landing pad. Report and submission dates below are distinct from publication dates.

**Kasungu District Hospital medical drone pickup**. Wingcopter identifies Kasungu District Hospital as a pickup point for medicines and vaccines distributed by drone. The existing Kasungu Aerodrome record describes a testing corridor, not this hospital. The operator documents the hospital pickup, not a dedicated pad, a site-level flight count or a current timetable. The map point represents the locality, not an exact launch or landing point. [Source](https://wsd.wingcopter.com/malawi-operations).

**Ntchisi District Hospital medical drone pickup**. Wingcopter identifies Ntchisi District Hospital as a pickup point for medicines and vaccines distributed by drone. The operator lists Ntchisi separately from Kasungu District Hospital. The operator documents the hospital pickup, not a dedicated pad, a site-level flight count or a current timetable. The map point represents the locality, not an exact launch or landing point. [Source](https://wsd.wingcopter.com/malawi-operations).

**Mbandaka EPI vaccine distribution centre**. VillageReach identifies the immunization programme’s Mbandaka distribution centre as the departure site for vaccine flights to Widjifake. Mbandaka airport coordinated traffic; it was not the reported dispatch site. This entry describes the 2019 trial, not current routine service. The map point represents the locality, not an exact launch or landing point. [Source](https://www.villagereach.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/DRC-Phase-1-Drone-Report_for-VR-website_with-deliverables.pdf).

**Widjifake health centre drone return site**. The health centre received vaccines by drone and dispatched specimens on a documented return flight to Mbandaka. Counted because the operator flew return cargo from this site. Downstream facilities reached by motorcycle are excluded. This entry describes the 2019 trial, not current routine service. The map point represents the locality, not an exact launch or landing point. [Source](https://www.villagereach.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/DRC-Phase-1-Drone-Report_for-VR-website_with-deliverables.pdf).

**Takara airstrip drone trial origin**. Swoop Aero logged outbound and return trial legs at Takara in December 2018. UNICEF identified the old airstrip as the test origin. These were trial flights. The record does not assert that patients received vaccinations at the airstrip or that a routine service followed. The map point represents the locality, not an exact launch or landing point. [Source](https://www.unicef.org/innovation/media/11951/file/Reference%203.7.%20Project%20Final%20Report_SWOOP%20AERO.pdf).

**Vaemali vaccine dispatch base**. Swoop Aero describes vaccine requests placed with its Vaemali base and fulfilled by drone for immunization visits. This is a dispatch base on Epi. Schools visited by nurses are not counted as separate sites. The previously listed Dillon’s Bay trial took place on Erromango. The map point represents the locality, not an exact launch or landing point. [Source](https://www.unicef.org/innovation/media/11951/file/Reference%203.7.%20Project%20Final%20Report_SWOOP%20AERO.pdf).
