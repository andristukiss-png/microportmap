# Tenth sourced expansion and status review

Reviewed 29 October 2026. Five additions bring the catalogue to 255 research records across 37 countries. Four existing Australian Wing records are explicitly corrected to historical; the other 246 prior records remain unchanged. The original 50-record snapshot remains intact.

## Counting decisions

Stonebriar Centre and Hulen Mall are separately named origins in Wing’s launch report, not one port per participating merchant. Denton South Loop 288 is distinct from the already catalogued planned Zipline University Drive store. Coppet is one laboratory rooftop station in a reported trial; the unnamed laboratory and its exact position remain unresolved. Ormeau is a historical pilot announcement, not evidence of current operation or confirmation of its scheduled implementation.

All map points are editorial locality references. These records do not establish landing locations, public access, aviation permission or independently verified current operations.

## Status corrections

Grand Plaza, Eastland, Hyperdome and Orion Springfield now carry historical status and a linked 28 May 2026 Australian Aviation report of Wing’s Australian delivery cessation. Original sources, publication dates, identities and location references are retained. The national cessation report does not establish individual shutdown dates or physical removal. Canberra was already historical and is unchanged.

The correction ledger stores complete before/after records. Tests accept only the exact reviewed changes to status, verification wording, review date, supporting sources and review notes. Prior preservation digests are checked against the original versions after validating these corrections; all other fields and records remain protected.
