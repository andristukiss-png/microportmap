# Batch 121: Japanese training networks

Reviewed 10 November 2026: 50 candidate decisions, 36 additions, nine deferred leads and five overlaps. Total 990 records in 56 countries; ten remaining to 1,000. All 954 prior records preserved.

The review covers 18 Drone Campus venues, 22 KDDI venue candidates (Sendai has separate indoor and outdoor sites), and ten previously unresolved leads. Accepted venues comprise 18 Drone Campus facilities, 15 KDDI venues and three follow-up sites.

Individual campus pages take precedence over national indexes: Hiroshima is mapped to Takehara rather than the older Etajima listing; Tokushima follows the detailed Hirono venue address while documenting the conflicting index. KDDI Kyoto school labels conflict with their named venues: the records follow Daigo Regional Gym in Fushimi and Kelbito in Nishikyo. Nagoya's venue is in Chita, and Matsuyama's in Toon. LIVERA's operator resolves the conflicting street number.

Shintotsukawa DRONE PLUS shares the already recorded Drone Sky Hub facility. Itabashi, Misato, Toyota and Suminoe also overlap existing records. Aizu and DMS Osaka have unresolved address conflicts. Chichibu's unnamed partner venues are not inferred from its office. Course levels, gyms and grounds on one campus are grouped; shared sports venues are not described as exclusive drone facilities.

All markers are approximate editorial locality references, not launch coordinates. Current operation, public access and flight permission are not independently verified. Source publication dates are not inferred from course calendars or opening dates.

Validation coverage: 995 browser routes / 1,990 desktop-mobile journeys. Actual CI and deployment results are recorded in the pull request.
