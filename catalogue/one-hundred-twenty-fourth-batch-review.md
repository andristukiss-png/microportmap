Indoor and outdoor areas on a single campus are grouped. Shared sports facilities are not described as exclusive drone infrastructure. Office and theory-only addresses are excluded. JULC Nagano's Iizuna Connect WEST address matches the existing Drone Campus Nagano record. Other Nagano venues still require practical-use confirmation.

New practical-course evidence resolves Ishige Gym, Chikuchiku Bonbon, Ebima, Yaizu Nakane Shinden and Sekido Joetsu. The Sekido operator explicitly distinguishes its Sanwa No training centre from its Fujinoshinden headquarters. This does not resolve the separate Sanwa Seibu Sports House lead. Historical course listings establish reported use, not current availability; future course listings do not establish that an event has occurred.

Follow-up leads include Sharp Tenri campus, Tamano Sports Centre, Saga's rural outdoor field, Kokubu Children Gym, Koriyama Tamura Community Centre, BANKYO Cultural Hall and the Shirako venue. The deferred western branches require named practical venues before addition.

Markers are rounded editorial locality references, not launch coordinates. Current operation, access and flight permissions remain unverified.
