# Sixteenth sourced expansion: multi-city delivery origins

Six distinct origins reviewed on 29 October 2026 bring the catalogue to 286 records across 39 countries. All 280 previous records remain unchanged.

## Counting and evidence decisions

- Xili Yitian Holiday Li: operator and university identify the same departure origin. This is separate from the earlier Holiday Plaza record. University recipients and later Baoneng joint supply do not create duplicate origins.
- Badaling: the source identifies Badaling Fandian as the departure site. Tower destinations, new routes and waste-return flights do not become extra origins. Only dated launch and later route evidence is claimed; no current service or aviation permission is inferred.
- Longteng Avenue: the restaurant operator explicitly describes a dedicated station outside the restaurant, with automated loading. This establishes a physical origin beyond mere merchant participation. The skatepark recipient and wider West Bund network are not counted again.
- Xin’an: the government describes an integrated building and rooftop departure facility, distinct from Shiyan. All floors and transfer functions count once. A March 2026 visit reports flight demonstrations; it does not independently verify every commercial service.
- Nancun: departure from the named distribution centre is observed in the report. The airline/operator is not established, so the ordering mini-program and education partners are not assigned as operators. The article displays 8 December 2024; the launch occurred on 7 December.
- Yifang Tiandi: government reports identify the commercial-area service and later departure facility. The complex is one origin, separate from Tianhong. Merchants, constituent malls and receiving cabinets are not additional facilities.

All records retain source-reported operation with current-operation limitations. Targeted later-status searches do not establish uninterrupted operation. Map points are editorial locality references. No fleet-wide model or exact landing position is inferred.
