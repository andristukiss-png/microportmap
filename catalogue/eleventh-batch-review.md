# Eleventh sourced expansion: healthcare trial origins

Five additions reviewed on 29 October 2026 bring the catalogue to 260 research records in 38 countries. All prior 255 records remain unchanged, including the four Australian status corrections from the previous release. Portugal is newly represented.

## Scope and evidence

All five additions retain trial status. The sources identify actual trial activity, but do not establish current routine operation. Each record counts one named campus, origin or hub. Destination hospitals, aircraft, airspace corridors and future infrastructure are not multiplied into extra records. Coordinates are editorial locality references, not facility or landing positions.

- Edinburgh BioQuarter: a project partner describes the launchpad and witnessed departure. The Royal Infirmary reference is the same campus; the Borders endpoint is not separately added.
- Stracathro: the March 2023 committee report (printed page 55 / PDF page 57) confirms earlier successful medical-sample flights. The local trial report supplies additional operational context. The report date is not represented as an independently established publication date.
- A-kwadraat: the operator explicitly names the Turnhout central hub and both aircraft types. The permanent network remains a future ambition.
- San Raffaele: CORDIS describes a hospital pharmacy-courtyard launch and surveillance demonstrations. One campus covers both uses. The hospital’s own report corroborates the demonstration.
- São João: the research partner reports a 2025 test flight. Its planned vertiport is not labelled built; earlier demonstrations at the same hospital would require overlap review rather than another record. A medical payload is not asserted for the flight.
