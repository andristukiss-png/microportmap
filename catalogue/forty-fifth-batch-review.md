The [facilities directory](https://autonomy.illinois.edu/facilities) has differing dimensions. Neither precise dimensions nor a precise location from the footer are inferred; Stock Pavilion is not included in this record.
- [CSU Christman Airfield](https://dronecenter.colostate.edu/christman-airfield-use/): one shared aviation research site. The [Drone Center](https://dronecenter.colostate.edu/) also identifies its Fort Collins context. Historical dates and reopening announcements do not establish publication dates or current access. No source-reported waiver is represented as an independently verified permission.

All primary pages are undated. All three points are broad editorial locality references. Current operations, access and flight permissions remain independently unverified.
