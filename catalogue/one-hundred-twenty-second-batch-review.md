# Batch 122: JUAVAC and ATCL physical venues

Reviewed 10 November 2026: 50 candidate decisions, 12 additions, 36 deferred leads and two overlaps. Total 1,002 source-linked records in 56 countries. The 1,000-record catalogue target is reached; this is not a count of independently verified operating ports. All 990 prior records are preserved.

The review pool comprises 41 JUAVAC school listings and nine ATCL campus or experience venues. Eleven JUAVAC-linked physical venues and DMS Osaka Juso are added. The ledger records one accepted venue per candidate; separately mentioned sites remain follow-up leads.

Office addresses are not substituted for flight venues: Sappa-do is in Takada, Sainichi indoor training is in Ageo, Niigata's hall is in Kizaki, Ishikawa uses Kigoyama, Nagano uses its Komoro practical-training campus, Shizuoka instruction is at Lopta in Fuji, and Osaka uses MAG Futsal Stadium. DMS Osaka Juso is confirmed at Tagawakita by the local operator, resolving the conflicting group-index address. MAG is distinct from Flight Salon Osaka.

JUAVAC Kyoto and Fujitaka refer to one former Kawabe school campus. Fukuoka South's gym and school ground form one campus. The Nagomi former-factory hall is one venue; arranged outdoor experiences are not additional maintained fields. Sports halls and shopping-mall facilities are shared venues, not exclusive drone infrastructure. Himeji and Hirakata overlap existing records. ATCL experience-session listings alone do not establish permanent flight-training sites.

Further venue review: Niigata's Shibata former Honden school site, Nagano's Saku venue, Akita's two campuses, Yamasa's Niimi/Tamano grounds and the unresolved JUAVAC physical venues. Sainichi's Omiya Kenpo field is already recorded.

All markers are rounded editorial locality references, not launch coordinates. Current operation, access and flight permission are not independently verified. Historical or undated descriptions do not establish present availability.
