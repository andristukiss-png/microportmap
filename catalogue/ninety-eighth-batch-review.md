# Moorock and Newcastle testing sites

Reviewed 7 November 2026. Two records bring the catalogue to 567 records across 53 countries.

Vodafone identifies Manna’s Moorock test facility as the location of a completed connectivity trial. Its retrieved page does not display a publication date, so the date field remains null. The facility is reported-operation, with present availability unverified.

RAPID’s project report explicitly dates tests at Newcastle Airfield to 5–6 April 2022. These are event dates, not an asserted page-publication date. The record is historical. The nearby bridge survey location is not counted separately.

Duplicate review covered all 565 existing records and aliases Moorock, Moorcock, Bellair, Ballycumber, Newcastle and RAPID. Moorock is distinct from Manna delivery bases; Newcastle is the County Wicklow airfield. Individual tests, equipment and partners are not additional records. Map points are broad locality references, not operational coordinates.
