# Isles of Scilly airbridge airfields

Reviewed 5 November 2026. Two historical trial sites bring the catalogue to 549 records across 53 countries.

Page 3 of the Windracers stakeholder letter documents a completed four-week return-flight trial. Its proposed Scottish routes are not treated as completed operations. The operator letter is hosted by the regulator but remains operator-authored evidence. It has no displayed publication date; the field remains null. The dated university announcement supplies 2021 context and St Mary’s control-centre evidence.

Duplicate review covered all 547 prior records. Land’s End is a separate mainland airport; its earlier flight to St Mary’s does not create another island-site record. The Maryland UROC record is unrelated. No unnamed inter-island destinations are added.
