# Fiftieth sourced expansion: Pisa, Denver and Haifa

Reviewed 1 November 2026. Three additions bring the catalogue to 391 records across 43 countries; 609 remain toward 1,000. All prior 388 records remain unchanged. Israel is newly represented by a research facility, not an independently verified operating public port.

## Source decisions

- [University of Pisa research directory](https://www.aut.ing.unipi.it/index.php/ricerca) identifies the RAASL flight room, protection net and Vicon tracking. [The department](https://www.dii.unipi.it/) gives a Pisa address. [Earlier experiments](https://www.mdpi.com/2218-6581/9/4/110) describe a DII LARS flight room; no second facility is inferred from the different acronym. Other robotics labs and listed aircraft are not extra sites. The directory is undated.
- [MSU Denver laboratory directory](https://www.msudenver.edu/aviation-aerospace/aerospace-and-aerospace-systems-laboratories/) explicitly lists an inhouse flight cage within the UAS laboratory. [The March 2024 curriculum guide](https://www.msudenver.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/23-24-AVS-Curriculum-Guidelines-TSv20.pdf), printed page 12, corroborates it. One facility is counted; simulators, courses, ground stations and other aviation laboratories are not additional ports. The department address establishes the city, not the cage room. The guide edition does not date the undated webpage or facility opening.
- [Technion infrastructure directory](https://ri.technion.ac.il/researchers-technologies/?sort%5B15%5D=asc) lists a flight room for drone and robot experiments under Kiril Solovey. [His page](https://kirilsol.github.io/) establishes Haifa and links to the [Multi-Robot Systems Lab](https://mrstechnion.github.io/). Zisapel 717 is explicitly treated as an office, not the flight room. The adjacent cars-and-drones listing is equipment, not another site. Dimensions and tracking hardware are unspecified. The directory is undated; its indexed row is visible with the supplied sort parameter.

Coordinates remain broad editorial locality references. Current operations, access and flight permissions are independently unverified. No publication dates are invented from guide editions, page copyrights or crawl timestamps.
