# FMCI Shannon drone port

Reviewed 6 November 2026. One record brings the catalogue to 562 records across 53 countries.

FMCI describes a dedicated drone testing port adjacent to Shannon Airport. Its undated facility page documents take-off and landing zones and research support. The Irish government separately dates the wider campus opening to 15 July 2022; this does not establish the drone port’s opening date. Earlier passenger-vertiport plans are not evidence of passenger service.

Duplicate review searched all 561 existing records for FMCI, Future Mobility, Shannon and Foynes. The port is counted once: landing zones, offices, fleet and campus facilities are not extra records. The map marker is a broad Shannon locality reference, not an operational coordinate. Current operation, bookings and access remain unverified.
