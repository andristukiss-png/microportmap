# Radnor Range drone test site

Reviewed 5 November 2026. One research facility brings the catalogue to 556 records across 53 countries.

The operator's undated service page describes drone testing at Radnor Range and explicitly prohibits public access. Its October 2019 demonstration reference is not treated as the page publication or facility opening date. Current operations and bookings have not been independently verified.

The contact page distinguishes the range from its administrative office in Presteigne. The May 2022 site information identifies Harley Dingle, New Radnor, Powys. The rounded marker is a locality reference, not an entrance, launch point or flight boundary.

Duplicate review covered all 555 prior records and aliases Radnor, New Radnor, Harley Dingle and Presteigne. The range is counted once; internal trial areas and service offerings do not add records.
