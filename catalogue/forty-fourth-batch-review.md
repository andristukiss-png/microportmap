# Forty-fourth sourced expansion: Long Beach, Seattle and Beavercreek

Reviewed 1 November 2026. Three additions bring the catalogue to 373 records across 41 countries; 627 remain toward 1,000. All prior 370 records remain unchanged.

## Source decisions

- [CSULB PACK Lab report](https://www.csulb.edu/news/article/csulb-students-train-autonomous-robots-for-real-world-rescue), published 24 November 2025: documents a built netted cage and optical tracking. The swarm, ground vehicles and indoor driving range are not extra drone sites. Proposed emergency applications and planned college modernization are not assumed deployed or completed.
- [UW Bowman testing report](https://www.aa.washington.edu/news/article/2020-11-23/peering-snow), published 23 November 2020: reports Argo testing before a March 2020 pandemic closure. That does not establish present closure or reopening. The [2019 university self-study](https://www.aa.washington.edu/files/about/ABET/2019_SelfStudy_ABET-FINAL_AA_06272019.pdf), printed page 67, describes the Bowman UAV arena near the Seattle campus. Mount Baker field experiments are not additional permanent facilities.
- [Matrix Research Drone Lab](https://www.matrixresearch.com/capabilities/drone-lab), undated: operator description of one outdoor cage with supporting building. The [facilities page](https://www.matrixresearch.com/about/facility) identifies Beavercreek, whereas its postal address says Dayton. These are treated as one locality context, not duplicate sites. No exact cage coordinate or independent confirmation of advertised pilot qualifications is asserted.

All three additions use approximate editorial locality points. Equipment, partners and institutional aliases do not increase facility counts. Current operations, access and flight permissions remain independently unverified.
