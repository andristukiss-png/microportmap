# Sixth fifty candidate-site screening tasks

Reviewed 8 November 2026: 50 candidate decisions, comprising 29 additions, 15 deferred candidates and six existing-site overlaps. The catalogue grows from 660 to 689 records across 53 countries, leaving 311 to the 1,000-record target.

This batch covers Japanese training, testing and recreational flight venues. Every accepted entry has operator or government evidence and a locality-level map reference. Courts, floors, plots and shared-campus facilities are grouped. These are source-reported research records, not operational inspections or guarantees of current access.

Important reconciliations: Sekido’s former field and renamed Yokohama training centre are one site; Muramoto’s Sakai building and Tondabayashi outdoor field are separate. Nose Kogen’s street number differs between two sources, so only Shukuno is used. ZOZOPARK is retained under the municipal directory name; its operator link now redirects to SMBC FIELD, and updated drone-use confirmation remains outstanding. Municipal Chiba test fields are research facilities, not general pilot-practice sites. Osaka sports-park rental leads are deferred rather than presented as dedicated drone installations.

TK Drone Park and GAP150 have readable primary-page search-index evidence but direct retrieval timed out or was blocked. Failed retrieval of other candidates does not establish closure. Original publication dates remain null except JMA’s explicitly dated 6 August 2023 report; municipal update dates are labelled as updates. Prices, schedules, legal exemptions and exact launch coordinates are not asserted.

All 660 earlier records are preserved. The machine-readable ledger is [2026-11-08-sixth-fifty-task-review.json](2026-11-08-sixth-fifty-task-review.json).
