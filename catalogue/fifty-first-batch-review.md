# Fifty-first sourced expansion: Hoboken and two Auckland campuses

Reviewed 1 November 2026. Three additions bring the catalogue to 394 records across 43 countries; 606 remain toward 1,000. All prior 391 records remain unchanged.

## Source decisions

- [Stevens SAS equipment](https://saslabstevens.github.io/equipment.html) identifies an enclosed flight arena, Vicon tracking and a Hoboken contact location. [The university report](https://www.stevens.edu/news/exploring-improved-drone-designs-defenses) corroborates the flying range and links to SAS; [the faculty profile](https://www.stevens.edu/profile/hjafarne) confirms the group affiliation. One range is counted. Other aircraft, ground robots and marine research are not added sites. The equipment page is undated; its copyright and the lab homepage’s dated news-link entry do not establish the equipment-page publication date.
- [DTRG facilities](https://dtrg.org/facilities/) explicitly separates the City Campus netted flight area from Newmarket. [University dynamics and control facilities](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/engineering/our-research/engineering-research/research-areas-and-facilities/dynamics-and-control.html) corroborate motion capture and drone research. One City Campus lab is counted, regardless of research-group aliases or shared use. The direct 2025 competition detail page returned 403, so it is not required evidence for this record.
- [The university’s 1 September 2026 wind-tunnel report](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/news/2026/09/01/my-space-wind-tunnel.html) explicitly describes drones flown inside the tunnel with motion capture. [Wind Tunnel Hall](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/engineering/our-research/engineering-research/research-areas-and-facilities/wind-tunnel-hall.html) establishes Newmarket; DTRG corroborates the separate facility. Other uses and sections are not extra drone sites. Sources disagree on dimensions and maximum speed, so no exact specification is adopted. The 2015 tunnel construction date is not the drone-testing start date.

Deferred: UTIAS dataset evidence alone does not establish a separate site from the existing Myhal record. McGill MILUV documents a flight arena, but its precise facility identity needs more review before inclusion.

All coordinates remain broad locality references. Current operation, access and flight permissions are independently unverified.

## Verification scope

Canonical hashes preserve all 341 prior additions; existing snapshots preserve the original 50 records and 72 HTML pages. Browser coverage includes 399 routes at two sizes (798 journeys). Checks cover distinct campus counting, source dates, exports and deterministic builds.
