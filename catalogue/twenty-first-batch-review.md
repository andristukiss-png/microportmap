# Twenty-first sourced expansion: research airfields and training origins

Reviewed 30 October 2026. Five additions bring the catalogue to 310 records across 39 countries, leaving 690 toward 1,000. All prior 305 records remain unchanged. These are research records, including a dated trial, not a count of currently operating commercial microports.

## Evidence and identity decisions

- NMSU’s airport UAS hangar, southeastern main-campus training field and the USDA Jornada airstrip are three explicitly distinguished physical locations. A shared research partner or airspace authorisation does not collapse them into one facility, nor create extra records for the wider airspace. University and USDA office addresses are not treated as launch sites.
- Jornada retains the dated 1 April 2014 research account. Repeated vegetation-imaging flights support source-reported research use; they do not establish present-day operations. Survey plots, aircraft types and ground-control equipment are not separate ports.
- Skyway 36 in Tulsa is distinct from the already recorded OSU Glencoe flight station. The wider Skyway programme, renovations and additional testing equipment do not create duplicate records.
- Hillsboro is labelled a trial origin. The explicit article publication date is 20 May 2016 despite its archive directory and breadcrumb saying 16 May. No exact first-flight date is inferred. Farm recipients, image-collection corridors and the statewide Northern Plains programme are not extra facilities.
- Eagle Field is deferred: the January 2026 partnership announcement alone does not establish implemented testing under the proposed authorisation.
- Undated NMSU facility pages retain null publication dates. All map points remain broad editorial locality references, not extracted facility or landing coordinates. Access and flight permissions are not established by a catalogue record.
