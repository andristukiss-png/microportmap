# Forty-third sourced expansion: Liverpool, Nottingham and Cranfield

Reviewed 1 November 2026. Three additions bring the catalogue to 370 records across 41 countries; 630 remain toward 1,000. All prior 367 records remain unchanged.

## Source decisions

- [Liverpool DIF Extreme Environment Lab](https://digitalinnovationfacility.com/facilities/extreme-environment-lab/): an undated university facility page describes a multi-storey indoor aerial/ground robot lab. The [university opening gallery](https://news.liverpool.ac.uk/2022/05/26/gallery-digital-innovation-facility-opening/) supports its place within DIF and describes the May 2022 opening event. Multiple storeys, the umbrella institute and unrelated DIF labs do not increase the drone site count. The event date is not used as the undated primary page publication date.
- [Nottingham Trent Robotics Test Arena](https://www.ntu.ac.uk/study-and-courses/courses/our-facilities/robotics-test-arena): an undated official page describes drone tracking and teaching/research equipment. The [Engineering Building facilities directory](https://www.ntu.ac.uk/study-and-courses/courses/our-facilities/engineering-building) links the same arena. Computer science and engineering listings are not separate sites; the university footer address and building opening year are not an arena address or opening date.
- [Cranfield design testing report](https://www.cranfield.ac.uk/press/news-2025/cranfield-emergency-flyer-design-through-to-next-stage-of-global-competition), published 10 December 2025: explicitly reports design testing in the Drone Flight Arena. Prototype aircraft, competition entrants and partners are not additional facilities. Future full-scale flight plans in December 2026 and at NASA Ames in 2027 are not treated as completed operations.

FlyART at Politecnico di Milano and UNSW Canberra LIRTA were excluded because they already have catalogue records. All three additions use locality-level editorial coordinates rather than inferred facility positions. Current operation, access and flight permissions remain independently unverified.

## Verification scope

Canonical hashes preserve all 317 prior additions; existing snapshots preserve the original 50 records and 72 HTML pages. Browser coverage includes 375 routes at two sizes (750 journeys). Checks cover source dates, distinct identity, exports and deterministic builds.
