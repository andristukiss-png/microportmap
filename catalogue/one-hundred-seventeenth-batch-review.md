Reviewed 9 November 2026: 50 candidate decisions, 36 additions and 14 deferred leads. Total 870 records in 54 countries; 130 remaining to 1,000. All 834 prior records preserved.

Taiwan contributes 27 locations and Australia nine. Huwei is historical: September reporting says flight use had stopped following lease expiry. A successor site is not inferred. Renamed CTBC and Taiwan Steel campuses count once. Ming Chuan Taoyuan and Kinmen are distinct campuses; Kinmen is in Jinsha. National Ilan University's Chengnan field is in Yilan City. Ankang farm is separate from NTU's main campus.

Each Australian sports ground is an operator-listed training venue, not an unrestricted public flying area. Alternative fields count separately when their venue identities differ. Examination rooms, campus subdivisions and course schedules do not create extra locations. Unresolved fields, classroom-only evidence and unconfigured scenic flight areas remain deferred.

All map points are rounded editorial locality references, not facility coordinates or launch points. Current operation, access and flight permission are not independently verified. Publication dates are recorded only when explicitly supported; examination, opening and update dates are not substituted. Build coverage: 875 browser routes / 1,750 desktop-mobile journeys. Actual CI, preview and production results belong in the pull request.
