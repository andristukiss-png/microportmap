The review pool contains 29 distinct DPA school-group candidates, 11 D-Academy alliance members, six additional DMS campuses and four Southeast Asian venues. Repeated school entries within the DPA list were reviewed once.

Akihabara provides simulation and theory, with practical flight training at other campuses, so it is deferred. Indoor halls, outdoor areas and course levels on the same campus are grouped. Off-site areas mentioned by CSON, BirdEye and Sogakkan are not inferred from headquarters coordinates. Rumpin is a shared airfield with reported research use, not a public flying area. Pameungpeuk remains deferred pending specific UAV evidence and campus identity resolution.

All markers are approximate editorial locality references. Current operation, access and flight permission are not independently verified. Source publication dates are not opening or course dates.
