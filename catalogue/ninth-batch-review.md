## Identity and status decisions

- Eight separately addressed Houston-area Walmart origins are distinct from the five January 2026 origins already catalogued. Both reviewed local reports identify the same eight additions. Four records cite each report; the full list was cross-checked between them.
- Locality coordinates are deliberately city-level editorial references. Multiple Houston records therefore share a map point; exact published street addresses do not imply surveyed landing coordinates.
- Denton University Drive uses the city's scheduled-launch notice and the state project filing. Neither a planned launch date nor a construction schedule proves operation. NBC DFW's 26 March report has a present-tense headline but also future-tense launch wording; the record remains announced pending clearer confirmation. The Loop 288 store and North Lakes Park demonstration are not added.
- Rowlett is one explicitly scoped Zipline programme, not one record per restaurant, aircraft, Dropbox or delivery destination. Future individually identified Rowlett origins must resolve overlap with this network before increasing the count.
- No source-page, runtime or palette changes are required. Existing originals and additions are protected by preservation checks.
