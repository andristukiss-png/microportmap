# Avonmouth NPAS drone trial base

Reviewed 6 November 2026. One historical trial site brings the catalogue to 557 records across 53 countries.

NPAS's undated retrospective reports July–December 2025 trials from Avonmouth Port. NATS AIP Supplement 094/2025 names Avonmouth Heliport as the operating base for the later phase. The dated NPAS launch report identifies the CAMCOPTER S-100. The retrospective is not assigned a publication date inferred from its contents.

Duplicate review covered all 556 prior records and aliases Avonmouth, NPAS, National Police Air Service and Severn Estuary. Port and heliport descriptions are counted as one trial origin. Airspace sectors and remote control partners do not add records. Existing Bristol university research sites are separate. No ongoing police drone service is inferred.

The marker is a rounded Avonmouth locality reference, not a helipad, access point or flight boundary. Current operation and access remain unverified.
