# Thirty-sixth sourced expansion: Kamenz and Delft

Reviewed 1 November 2026. Three additions bring the catalogue to 349 records across 41 countries; 651 remain toward 1,000. All prior 346 records are unchanged.

## Source decisions

- [MITEB opening report](https://www.aero.polimi.it/it/magazine/daer-participation-in-grand-opening-of-miteb-hangar-for-airship-research-kamenz-germany), published 22 October 2026: one hangar supporting unmanned-airship experimentation. The [city account](https://www.kamenz.de/ausfuehrliche-nachricht/wenn-science-fiction-zur-realitaet-wird.html) resolves the university article’s inconsistent state name in favour of Saxony. Opening and publication dates differ. Manufacturing plans are not treated as completed production.
- [Mobile Robotics Lab](https://research.tudelft.nl/en/equipments/mobile-robotics-lab/): the [research group](https://autonomousrobots.nl/robots/) locates its arena in Mechanical Engineering’s F-wing. This is distinct from the existing Cyberzoo in the Aerospace Aircraft Hall.
- [Swarming Lab](https://www.tudelft.nl/sciencecentre/maken/robotlab/swarming-lab): the [university facilities directory](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/ae/organisation/departments/control-and-operations/facilities/drone-facilities) lists it separately from Cyberzoo. Do not count participating organisations, individual robots, proposed exhibitions or future equipment as more facilities. Both Delft primary pages are undated.

Valkenburg was already catalogued and was not added again. The RoboFactory directory entry says coming soon and was not included in this batch. Approximate city points are not building, launch or landing coordinates; present operation and public access remain unverified.

## Verification scope

Canonical hashes preserve all 296 previous additions; existing snapshots preserve the original 50 records and 72 HTML pages. Browser coverage includes 354 routes at two sizes (708 journeys). Checks cover exports, sources, identities and deterministic builds.
