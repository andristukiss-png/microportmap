# Fourth fifty candidate-site screening tasks

Reviewed 8 November 2026. Completed 50 further candidate screenings: 11 accepted records, 26 deferred candidates and 13 overlaps excluded. The catalogue reaches 640 research records across 53 countries, leaving 360 to the target of 1,000.

Each task screens one candidate for evidence and distinct physical scope. A completed screening can defer addition. The machine-readable ledger is [2026-11-08-fourth-fifty-task-review.json](2026-11-08-fourth-fifty-task-review.json).

The accepted records describe reported test infrastructure. Current operation, access, bookings and flight permission are not independently verified. Rounded locality markers are not surveyed launch points or flight boundaries. Undated facility descriptions retain null publication dates; years of activity and construction are not substituted for publication dates.

Counting review covered all 629 existing records and site aliases. Marulan is distinct from Sydney’s indoor laboratory. INTA explicitly locates CEDEA approximately ten kilometres from CEUS. Aviolanda’s adjoining Dronelab and airbase facilities are grouped, excluding the separately listed Deelen site. BCN Drone Center is at Moià, not central Barcelona. Skellefteå’s droneport is distinct from its airport and educational campus. Västervik’s conflicting airspace-area figures are omitted.

Deferred leads include regional airspace without a resolved ground base, future or uncertain development phases, component-testing laboratories, project announcements and unspecified operating locations. Actual offshore drone testing at Atlantis is supported, but its record boundary and launch-base identity need further work. Optand’s cited authorization ended in June 2025 and is not presented as current. Overlap exclusions retain established campus counting boundaries.

All 579 earlier additions and 50 original records are preserved. Automated checks reconcile all 50 decisions with release membership and verify preservation. Planned browser coverage is 645 routes at desktop and mobile sizes, totaling 1,290 journeys. Actual test and production results are recorded in the pull request and GitHub Actions.
