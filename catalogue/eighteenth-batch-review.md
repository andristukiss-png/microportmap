# Eighteenth sourced expansion

Reviewed 30 October 2026. Four additions bring the catalogue to 294 records across 39 countries, leaving 706 toward 1,000. All 290 previous records remain unchanged.

## Identity and scope decisions

- Futian COCO Park is explicitly identified as a departure area in the reporter’s exchange with Meituan. It is separate from Bantian Galaxy WORLD and the municipal traffic-dock network. Courier fallback is retained as a service limitation.
- Capriotti’s is explicitly reported to have a rooftop dock. It is not counted merely because a restaurant sells through a delivery platform. Its general Frisco–Little Elm location is retained; the restaurant directory is supporting context and does not independently prove the dock’s installation. Publication date is confirmed by the publisher’s article archive.
- Longgang’s upgraded test base retains its earlier identity. Phase two and proposed certification centres do not add sites.
- The Dayun park’s own small-drone field is a separate physical site: district documentation places the larger test base about five kilometres away. Shared programme affiliation and locality map points do not merge the facilities.

Longgang Vanke remains deferred: government and research sources identify the commercial-area service, but the reviewed evidence did not clearly identify its physical departure facility. No record was added solely to meet a batch size.
