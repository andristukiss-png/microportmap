# Enschede, Oeiras and Aldenhoven

Reviewed 2 November 2026. Three distinct sites take the catalogue to 406 records; 594 remain to reach 1,000.

**Twente Airport unmanned flight test site** — Twente Airport describes an airport test environment for experimental drones at Technology Base. The airport site is separate from the university ITC UAV Lab. Space53 is a cluster associated with the airport, not another site. The demonstration programme describes a fictitious hospital and simulated missions; neither is evidence of a hospital port or routine deliveries. The dated integration of Space53 is not the facility-page publication date. Coordinates represent the locality only. Current operation, access and flight permission are not independently verified. [Primary source](https://www.twente-airport.nl/en/unmanned-systems).

**Técnico Taguspark flight arena** — Técnico reported drone-system testing in the flight arena on its Taguspark campus during the FirePuma research project. This is the Taguspark campus arena in Oeiras, distinct from the IDMEC Robotics Arena recorded in Lisbon. The 2022 article distinguishes tests in the arena from successful simulation and planned field flights. It does not establish a deployed wildfire surveillance service. Coordinates represent the locality only. Current operation, access and flight permission are not independently verified. [Primary source](https://tecnico.ulisboa.pt/pt/noticias/projeto-do-tecnico-ajuda-a-vigiar-incendios/).

**Aldenhoven Center for Vertical Mobility** — A March 2026 report documents the start of construction of a permanent vertical-mobility research centre beside the Aldenhoven Testing Center. The report was published on 20 March and dates the groundbreaking to 17 March. Construction does not establish commissioning or current flight operations at the permanent facility. The adjacent automotive testing centre is a separate site; temporary infrastructure and planned components are not counted as additional ports. Coordinates represent the locality only. Current operation, access and flight permission are not independently verified. [Primary source](https://www.aldenhoven-testing-center.de/en/blog/groundbreaking-ceremony-for-the-center-for-vertical-mobility-cvm-in-aldenhoven.html).
