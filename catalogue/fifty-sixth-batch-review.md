# Central Florida proposed drone delivery nests

Reviewed 2 November 2026. [FAA final assessment](https://www.faa.gov/sites/faa.gov/files/06272025_Final_EA_Wing_Central_Florida.pdf), Table 2.2-1 (p. 2-3), lists seven initial sites. Six new addresses are included:

- 990 Missouri Ave N, Largo, Florida, United States
- 11110 Causeway Blvd, Brandon, Florida, United States
- 2601 James L Redman Pkwy, Plant City, Florida, United States
- 8990 Turkey Lake Rd, Orlando, Florida, United States
- 11250 E Colonial Dr, Orlando, Florida, United States
- 2855 N Old Lake Wilson Rd, Kissimmee, Florida, United States

1450 Johns Lake Road, Clermont, is already recorded as wing-walmart-2695. The existing Brandon record at 1208 E Brandon Boulevard is a different address. The two Orlando stores are distinct even though their approximate city markers coincide.

The May 2025 assessment specifies no publication day on its cover. The [regional decision](https://www.faa.gov/uas/advanced_operations/nepa_and_drones/20250530_Wing_Central_FL_EA_FONSI-ROD_Signed.pdf) has a separate signature date. Neither establishes opening at an individual store. No unnamed potential future sites, delivery destinations or service radii have been counted.
