# India: clinical cargo flights

Reviewed 2 November 2026. Seven named health facilities are documented as origins of cargo. All seven describe past events. No record is added solely because a site received a delivery, hosted a launch ceremony or appeared on a list of partners.

**Keylong Regional Hospital drone trial**. The study records medicines sent from Keylong Regional Hospital to Tholang Primary Health Centre. The separate Kachrang dry run is not counted as a medical delivery. The marker represents the locality only; it does not identify a launch pad or establish current access or service. [Primary source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11816105/).

**Shansha Community Health Centre drone trial**. The study records a drone taking medicines from Shansha to Tholang, including iron–folic acid and an antibiotic. This is the Shansha departure on a distinct route, not another name for the Keylong hospital. The marker represents the locality only; it does not identify a launch pad or establish current access or service. [Primary source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11816105/).

**Tholang Primary Health Centre drone trial**. Tholang sent diagnostic material back on the Keylong route and dispatched medicines to Jhalma in the same feasibility study. The study records sputum and blood samples on the return leg. Both roles belong to one facility. The marker represents the locality only; it does not identify a launch pad or establish current access or service. [Primary source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11816105/).

**Jhalma Primary Health Centre drone trial**. Table 2 records medicines delivered from Tholang to Jhalma and diagnostic reports carried on the return leg. It is the documented return of reports, not receipt of medicines, that supports this record. No specimen shipment from Jhalma is inferred. The marker represents the locality only; it does not identify a launch pad or establish current access or service. [Primary source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11816105/).

**Sonipat eye hospital corneal tissue dispatch**. The ministry reports that a drone carried corneal tissue from the Sonipat centre of Dr. Shroff’s Charity Eye Hospital to the National Cancer Institute at AIIMS Jhajjar. The completed shipment establishes Sonipat as an origin. The study also considered amniotic grafts, but this record does not assert that one was flown on this trip. The marker represents the locality only; it does not identify a launch pad or establish current access or service. [Primary source](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2114748&lang=2&reg=48).

**Karkala Dr. TMA Pai Rotary Hospital tissue dispatch**. ICMR documents the aircraft taking off at Dr. TMA Pai Hospital in Karkala with a specimen bound for KMC Manipal. The university identifies the collaborating Karkala facility as Dr. TMA Pai Rotary Hospital. The inauguration venue in Manipal is not treated as the departure site. The marker represents the locality only; it does not identify a launch pad or establish current access or service. [Primary source](https://idrone.icmr.org.in/).

**Batahidia Char health camp sample dispatch**. At the 10 December 2024 health camp, patient samples collected at Batahidia Char Primary Health Centre were flown to AIIMS Guwahati for testing. The camp was held on 10 December; the ministry published its account the following day. AIIMS Guwahati already has a separate programme record and is not added again. The marker represents the locality only; it does not identify a launch pad or establish current access or service. [Primary source](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2083266&lang=2&reg=48).
