# First expansion batch — source review, 28 October 2026

These are ten new research records, not ten independently verified current operating facilities. Sources are linked in each JSON record and generated passport. The source-reading date is separate from the source's publication date and from the date of a reported event.

| Records | Evidence used | Counting decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Omenako; Mpanya | Ghana Ministry of Health launch/commissioning reports | Two separately named distribution centres; displayed publication dates were unavailable, so no date is invented. |
| Vobsi; Kete-Krachi | Zipline, 11 September 2023 | Two named centres; not 550 health-facility destinations. Vobsi is represented only at the Walewale locality scale. |
| Muhanga; Kayonza | Zipline, 15 December 2022 | Two named centres; not a record for every clinic or the whole national network. |
| EOC Lugano | Matternet, 30 September 2021 | One named Station deployment; the report describes a trial before replacing human handling. |
| Labor Berlin | Matternet authorization announcement, December 2023 | One announced network; state-level clearance was still pending in that source. Thirteen intended hospitals are not thirteen ports. |
| WakeMed Raleigh | Hospital report, 28 March 2019 | One campus programme; current operation is not inferred from the 2019 launch. |
| Wake Forest Baptist | Hospital report, 14 July 2020 | One central-campus medical delivery hub; the two initial routes are not two extra hubs. |

Descriptions are short paraphrases. Equipment models are only recorded when supported by the cited source. The six Zipline records deliberately leave the model unspecified. Matternet M2 is recorded where the source identifies it.

All coordinates are editorial references for a town or city, not coordinates extracted from the source, surveyed locations, pad locations, access instructions or aviation permissions. They must not be used for navigation. The locality wording is in `locationPrecision` and `coordinateBasis`.

The original 50 records were checked for duplicate IDs and named locations/operators. None of these ten is already represented. Entity keys and same-name/locality guards help prevent accidental reimport; manual review remains necessary for renamed operators, nearby sites and overlapping networks.

This batch starts the expansion pipeline. Reaching 1,000 or 10,000 requires continued independent source research, not replicating these records or converting service destinations into ports.
