# Twenty-ninth sourced expansion: DTU ASTA

Reviewed 31 October 2026. One addition brings the catalogue to 333 records across 40 countries, leaving 667 toward 1,000. All earlier 332 records remain unchanged.

## Evidence and identity

The [official DTU ASTA page](https://asta.dtu.dk/) describes an indoor research arena for aerial, ground and aquatic systems in Kongens Lyngby. Its [usage page](https://asta.dtu.dk/Facility-usage) documents research and teaching uses. The open space, tracking area and pool are one facility, not three ports. Research courses and individual robots are not sites. Reported-operation records the university description; present operation and access are not independently inspected.

The [older DTU CCAS page](https://ccas.dtu.dk/facilities/asta) supports identity and scope, but its 14 October 2020 update date is not used as an original publication date. The current primary and usage pages are undated. The map point is a broad Kongens Lyngby locality reference, not a geocoded address or landing coordinate.

## Duplicate review

ASTA is distinct from the existing UAS Denmark test centre near Odense. [SDU's airport Flight Lab](https://www.sdu.dk/en/forskning/sduuascenter/aboutsduuascenter/sdu-flight-lab) is explicitly part of that airport test centre, so it is not added as another site in this batch. UZH's general robotics page does not establish a sufficiently specific new facility identity and was not converted into a record.
