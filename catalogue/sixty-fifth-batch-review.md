# Norwegian dispatch sites

Reviewed 3 November 2026. Five origins checked against all prior records. The earlier Trondheim entry is a SINTEF research laboratory, not the Kyte delivery hub. The Røros project describes both a home-care facility and a hospital base; it is counted once. Sjusjøen, the two Coop delivery areas and the Troll A destination are not additional dispatch sites here. Locations are approximate.

- [Aviant Ski Airport delivery base](https://jobs.aviant.no/jobs/8325241-driver-for-drone-deliveries): The report identifies the airport but not a public collection point or a specific pad.
- [Kyte Trondheim home delivery hub](https://www.aviant.no/project/kyte): The hub is described at city level. The listed restaurant partners are not additional drone bases; the separate SINTEF laboratory is not this delivery project.
- [Kyte Lillehammer delivery base](https://www.shifter.no/nyheter/drone-grnderne-flyr-inn-mat-og-snus-til-hyttefolket/311750): The report locates the base near the ski jumps; it does not identify a precise pad or establish service after that ski season.
- [Røros home-care drone dispatch base](https://greenflyway.no/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Drone-project-report2025.pdf): The report also names Øverhagaen in describing the routes. Only one Røros origin is counted, with no claim about the precise launch building.
- [Mongstad offshore drone trial origin](https://www.equinor.com/news/archive/20200828-drone-transport-troll): Troll A is identified as the delivery destination. This record concerns the onshore origin and does not establish a scheduled offshore service.
