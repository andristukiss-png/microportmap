# Portland Drone Certification Agency test centre

Reviewed 5 November 2026. One research facility brings the catalogue to 555 records across 53 countries.

HeliOperations reports the facility launch on 17 April 2026 at Portland Heliport. The CAA separately lists the Drone Certification Agency at Osprey Quay among its pre-assessed UAS test sites. That listing supports site identity, not permission for any particular flight. Current operations and availability have not been independently inspected.

Duplicate review covered all 554 existing records and aliases Portland, Osprey Quay, Drone Certification Agency and HeliOperations. One heliport-based centre is counted. Maritime test areas and supporting services are not additional sites. The rounded northern Portland marker is not a runway position or operational boundary.
