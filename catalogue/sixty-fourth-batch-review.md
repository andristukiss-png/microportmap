# Swedish defibrillator drone stations

Reviewed 2 November 2026. The [regional health authority](https://www.vgregion.se/ov/innovationsplattformen/varden/innovationsprojekt/dronare2/pagaende-projekt/prehospitala-projekt/) names five places where it stationed drones. The [feasibility study](https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehab498) also maps three of those hangars. Its remote control centre at Säve Airport is not included as an additional dispatch site. The sixth site is a separate [operator-documented hangar in Östersund](https://everdrone.com/news-article/everdrone-emade-system-live-and-ready-to-help-save-lives-in-ostersund-sweden). Patient locations, catchment populations and emergency calls are not sites. Locality coordinates are not suitable for navigation. Checked names and aliases against the preceding catalogue.
