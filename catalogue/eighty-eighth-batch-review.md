# Swindon DroneTEX testing centre

Reviewed 5 November 2026. One research facility brings the catalogue to 553 records across 53 countries.

The Ministry of Defence records the opening on 12 June 2026. Panattoni's 24 June report identifies its Swindon park and indoor testing role. Source-reported opening is retained separately from current operational verification.

Duplicate review covered all 552 prior records, including aliases DroneTEX, Uncrewed Systems Centre, Swindon and Panattoni. The centre is counted once. The Tekever Spectrum factory and proposed Wroughton testing activity are separate sites and were not added. The map marker represents Swindon generally, not a building, entrance or flight boundary. Current operation, booking availability and public access remain unverified.
