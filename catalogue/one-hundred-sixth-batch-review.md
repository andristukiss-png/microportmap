# Second fifty candidate-site screening tasks

Reviewed 8 November 2026. Completed 50 further candidate screenings: 18 accepted records, 24 deferred candidates and eight overlapping entries excluded. The catalogue reaches 612 research records across 53 countries, leaving 388 to the target of 1,000.

Each task evaluates one lead for aerial testing evidence and distinct site scope. “Completed” means a screening decision is recorded; deferred candidates are not verified facilities. The machine-readable ledger is [2026-11-08-second-fifty-task-review.json](2026-11-08-second-fifty-task-review.json). This batch is separate from the earlier fifty-task review.

The additions document reported research and training infrastructure. Current operation, access, bookings and flight permission remain independently unverified. Coordinates are rounded editorial locality references. Publication dates are recorded only when displayed: the UPC English report was published on 13 December 2022 after the November opening; GIPSA’s page is dated 24 March 2026. Other dates, including course schedules, opening years and page revisions, are not treated as publication dates. TU Graz evidence concerns the 2024–25 course, not a newly verified service.

Site-scope review covers all 594 previous names, IDs, operators and candidate aliases. Indoor/outdoor areas on a training campus are grouped. The Aachen cage is distinct from Aldenhoven; Osaka Institute of Technology’s facility is in Hirakata; JBNU’s room is in Jeonju; GIPSA is in Saint-Martin-d’Hères. Imperial’s named Vicon arena is counted once without assigning it to a specific campus or independently counting other arena labels. Imperial evidence was available in indexed university content while direct retrieval returned 403. Limited retrieval and unresolved overlap prevented adding the Luxembourg facilities. IITRAM’s prospective wording does not establish completed infrastructure.

All 544 earlier additions and 50 original records are preserved. The expansion check verifies their preservation and reconciles the ledger against release membership. Browser coverage comprises 617 routes at desktop and mobile sizes: 1,234 journeys. Test outcomes are recorded in the pull request and GitHub Actions, not inferred from this coverage plan.
