- [Sheffield Sound and Motion Capture Lab](https://sheffield.ac.uk/sheffieldrobotics/kroto-research-institute-flying-lab): undated facility description explicitly supports small drones. Camera counts differ (24 versus 22), so no exact current inventory is asserted. The Mining Block equipment entry is not another drone site without flight evidence.
- [Sheffield Diamond Aerospace Lab](https://sheffield.ac.uk/engineering/diamond-engineering/our-facilities/aerospace-lab-simulation-propulsion): undated page identifies Room 1.05 and a physical netted flight-training area. This is distinct from the Kroto building. Simulators, aircraft and propulsion equipment are not additional locations.
- [Manchester transport showcase](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/about/news/manchester-transport-research-and-innovation/), published 5 October 2026: reports a live drone demonstration in the Aerospace Systems Laboratory indoor tracking arena. Other facilities on the tour are not drone sites. The panel venue and university footer do not establish the arena room.

York Robotics Laboratory was considered but direct university page reads returned 403; it is deferred. Buffalo SOAR and Maryland F3 are already catalogued and were excluded as duplicates.

City coordinates are approximate editorial references, not facility locations or landing points. Shared Sheffield coordinates do not imply co-location. Current operations, access and permissions remain independently unverified. Unknown publication dates remain null.
