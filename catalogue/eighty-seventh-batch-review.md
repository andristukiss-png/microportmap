# South Terras radiation survey

Reviewed 5 November 2026. One historical research site brings the catalogue to 552 records across 53 countries.

The peer-reviewed study reports five survey flights in 2022 and shows their takeoff and landing positions. Publication on 28 June 2023 is kept separate from flight timing. One mine-area site is counted, not five flight sectors or multiple historical studies. No permanent drone infrastructure is inferred.

Duplicate review covered all 551 prior records. South Terras is distinct from Fenswood Farm, Land’s End Airport and the separately reported St Just coastal trials. The latter were not added in this batch. The rounded mine-area marker is not a flight boundary or launch position; current operations and access remain unverified.
