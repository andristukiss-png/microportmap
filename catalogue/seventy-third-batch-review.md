# Four named dispatch origins in Japan

Reviewed 4 November 2026. Three historical trials and one disaster response. Map points represent approximate localities.

- [Yoshinoya Komatsu restaurant dispatch trial](https://aeronext.co.jp/news/yoshinoyapoc-komatsucity/): The restaurant was the dispatch origin, distinct from the previously recorded Honobono Shoto depot. The boathouse received the meals.
- [Tateishi Park hospital meal dispatch trial](https://aeronext.co.jp/news/yokosuka_flyingbeefbowl/): The food truck prepared the meals and the hospital received them. The park is separate from the LIVIN Yokosuka island dispatch site.
- [Kawazu Construction materials warehouse relief exercise](https://nextdelivery.aeronext.co.jp/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/97cdaf4f32b9bd5919a64bb96443b897-1.pdf): This was a disaster drill, not a delivery of actual medicine. The separate aerial survey is not counted as another dispatch site.
- [Yanagida Gymnasium earthquake relief dispatch](https://aeronext.co.jp/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/%E3%80%90%E5%88%A5%E6%B7%BB%E8%B3%87%E6%96%99%E3%80%91%E4%BB%A4%E5%92%8C6%E5%B9%B4%E8%83%BD%E7%99%BB%E5%8D%8A%E5%B3%B6%E5%9C%B0%E9%9C%87%E3%81%AB%E3%81%8A%E3%81%91%E3%82%8B%E3%83%89%E3%83%AD%E3%83%BC%E3%83%B3%E9%96%A2%E9%80%A35%E7%A4%BE%E3%81%AE%E5%88%9D%E6%9C%9F%E7%81%BD%E5%AE%B3%E6%99%82%E6%94%AF%E6%8F%B4%E6%B4%BB%E5%8B%95%E3%81%AB%E3%81%A4%E3%81%84%E3%81%A6.pdf): The gymnasium was a temporary relief origin in Noto Town, separate from the documented Wajima flights. Four trips count once.
