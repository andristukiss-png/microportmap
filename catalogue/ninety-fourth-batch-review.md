# West Wales Airport and West Freugh

Reviewed 6 November 2026. Two records bring the catalogue to 561 records across 53 countries.

West Wales Airport: TEKEVER describes testing since 2023 and its airport acquisition. The operator's own news index dates the report to 18 July 2025. Expansion plans are not treated as completed facilities or additional records.

West Freugh: Dstl's 26 May 2015 report documents UAS trials that month. QinetiQ independently identifies the location near Stranraer. The record retains historical scope without inferring present operations or precise flight dates.

Duplicate review covered all 559 existing records and aliases West Wales, Aberporth, Blaenannerch, Ceredigion, West Freugh and Stranraer. Each site is counted once, not by project, hangar, sortie or internal test area. The airport is distinct from Llanbedr and Predannack; its ownership does not merge it with other TEKEVER-associated locations. Map markers are broad locality references, not operational coordinates. Current operation and access remain unverified.
