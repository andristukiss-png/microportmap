# Eighth sourced expansion batch

Thirty delivery-related additions bring the catalogue to 240 research records. All prior 210 records are preserved. The new batch contains 18 historical DroneUp retail origins, two Zipline retail origins, one former Wing warehouse, five Canadian medical/logistics origins or programmes, two European medical trials and two French parcel terminals.

## Scope and duplicate review

- The 18 DroneUp entries have distinct published store addresses. They document historical services, not current delivery availability or surviving physical equipment. The later WHRO report records the end of the Walmart partnership. No exact shutdown day is inferred for each individual store.
- Clermont stores #2695 and #5299 are deliberately excluded because the catalogue already represents those physical origins under Wing. A provider change does not create another site.
- Only four of the eleven origins in the Texas release are added in this batch. Unreviewed entries do not count as completed research. The three Garland addresses are explicitly distinct.
- Mesquite and Waxahachie are the two P2 origins named by Walmart. The Mesquite Belt Line Road origin is separate from the unadded DroneUp Town East Boulevard site. The wider Wing rollout is not evidence of additional Zipline origins.
- Canberra's Mitchell warehouse is historical following reported service cessation. Earlier Bonython trials are not counted as additional Mitchell sites.
- The Edmonton airport record counts one central DroneSpot, not its Nisku and clinic endpoints. Later route extension and aircraft replacement do not create a new airport base.
- Beausoleil is represented by its Cedar Point origin. Georgina Island remains one network because its mainland origin is not individually resolved. These are distinct Indigenous community programmes.
- DSV Milton and Milton District Hospital are separate named physical origins. Oakville is a shared destination and is not separately counted in this batch.
- Svendborg is conservatively a trial. Biogroup explicitly describes its Bourgoin-Jallieu project as testing, despite the supplier's description of daily flights. Neither repeated flights nor an expired permit is proof of current clinical service.
- The French DPD entries represent different locations and routes: Saint-Maximin's secured origin terminal and Mont-Saint-Martin's explicitly documented fixed receiving terminal. A mobile van is not counted as a fixed port.
- Coordinates are editorial locality references. Exact source addresses do not make the map points surveyed landing positions. Dates are publication dates when displayed; the Virginia report keeps a null day rather than inventing one.
