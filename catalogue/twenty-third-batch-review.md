# Twenty-third sourced expansion: Asian test campuses and a historical origin

Reviewed 30 October 2026. Five additions bring the catalogue to 321 records across 40 countries, leaving 679 toward 1,000. All prior 316 records remain unchanged.

## Identity, timing and status decisions

- Fukushima: the RTF overview and two-site brochure distinguish Minamisoma’s campus from the Namie runway site. These are two physical sites within one programme. Individual runways, hangars, netted enclosures, robot areas and the connecting corridor are not extra ports. The prefecture records integration into F-REI on 1 April 2025; this is reflected in institutional attribution rather than carrying forward an older operator assumption. Older FIPO English facility URLs redirect, so the accessible RTF overview is the principal link. A detailed aerial-area link returned a redirect loop during this review; no uptime claim is made for it.
- Taiki: one runway-based UAV research facility. The 4 October 2026 operator release establishes SPACE COTAN’s concession transition effective 1 October and describes earlier drone use. Older JAXA material describes the same aerospace-park context and a shorter runway. Extension, renaming, hangars and rocket-launch developments are not additional drone ports. No operational rocket-launch claim is inferred from a future launch-complex plan.
- Goheung: one KARI flight-test campus with UAV facilities. Multiple runways and expansion phases remain components. It is geographically and institutionally distinct from KIAST’s Goseong centre. Differing historical construction dates across summaries are not used as publication dates or as evidence of extra facilities.
- PIXEL: the 25 October 2024 CAAS release identifies the actual origin of July test flights. The record is historical because that decision excluded flight testing and demonstrations from H3 Dynamics’ permitted activities. This is not a claim that PIXEL, the whole one-north estate, or every operator ceased operation. The accident destination is not counted. No later enforcement outcome or current permission is inferred.
- The Goseong development centre is deferred because its relationship to the already recorded Goseong flight-test campus needs facility-level reconciliation. The Maritime Drone Estate is deferred pending reconciliation with the existing Singapore waterfront dock records.

Undated facility pages retain null publication dates. All points are broad editorial locality references, not extracted runway or landing coordinates. Current operations, access and flight permissions remain independently unverified.
