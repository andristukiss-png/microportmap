# Third fifty candidate-site screening tasks

Reviewed 8 November 2026. Completed 50 further candidate screenings: 17 accepted records, 24 deferred candidates and nine overlaps excluded. The catalogue reaches 629 research records across 53 countries, leaving 371 to the target of 1,000.

Each task screens one candidate for evidence and distinct physical scope. Completed screening does not mean a deferred candidate is verified. This review is separate from the previous two fifty-task reviews. The machine-readable ledger is [2026-11-08-third-fifty-task-review.json](2026-11-08-third-fifty-task-review.json).

Accepted records cover reported flight, testing and training infrastructure. None establishes current operation, access, bookings or flight permission. Rounded locality markers are not surveyed launch points or flight boundaries. Source publication dates are retained only when displayed; page revisions, course schedules, event dates and construction dates are not substituted.

Counting review covered all 612 previous records, source aliases and campus scope. Singapore’s Pandan and Dover areas are distinct. Nagoya Port South District 5 is in Chita. Kawachi’s operator explicitly pauses new annual-use applications; Miho has interim facilities without hangars, electricity or water. Conflicting Hashio area figures are omitted. Gimcheon’s detailed facility page reports December 2023 completion, while an older overview snippet still labels it planned. Sejong’s indoor room is counted alone, without adding its separately described rooftop. The DJI opening report is from 2016 and does not establish a present service.

Deferred entries include simulator-only or unlocated laboratory leads, an upcoming November workshop, temporary event cages, unresolved institutional identity and site overlap, and sources with retrieval limits. Sakai’s completed ACSL site needs boundary reconciliation with the existing depot-led record. Already represented university arenas and Hokkaido Spaceport are excluded, including CMU Hazelwood and Pennovation.

All 562 earlier additions and 50 original records are preserved. Automated checks verify preservation and reconcile accepted IDs with release membership. Planned browser coverage is 634 routes at desktop and mobile sizes, totaling 1,268 journeys; actual test results are recorded in the pull request and GitHub Actions.
