# Scottish hospital flight trials

Reviewed 5 November 2026. Two named hospital trial sites bring the catalogue to 538 records in 53 countries. The Scottish Ambulance Service records CAELUS2 ending in December 2024. Both new records are historical and use rounded locality markers.

- Borders General Hospital is supported by the project partner's explicit report of flights to and from the hospital. The existing Edinburgh BioQuarter entry covers the opposite campus. Cargo descriptions apply to the trial as a whole.
- Crosshouse is the mainland origin named in the project lead's report. The blood-product scenario was simulated. The evidence reviewed does not justify counting Arran as a separate return-cargo origin.
