# Telangana study sites and Mokokchung trial origin

Reviewed 2 November 2026. [Figure 1 and methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12991481/) identify 11 peripheral primary health centres participating in the specimen transport intervention. Names and groupings were read directly from the original figure, not inferred from the district facility list. The four diagnostic hubs, unnamed subcentres and already-listed Bibinagar command centre are not counted. Counts and performance outcomes are not allocated to individual sites. Coordinates are editorial locality approximations.

- Alair: Gundala, Rajapet.
- Bhongir: Thurkapally, Bommalaramaram, Kondamadugu, Bollepally.
- Choutuppal: Pochampally, Narayanpur.
- Ramannapet: Varkatpally, Munipampula, Addagudur.

[Nagaland DIPR](https://ipr.nagaland.gov.in/icmr-drone-resource-and-outreach-launched-mokokchung) identifies the CMO office as the launch point to Longsa. The [ICMR annual report](https://www.icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/custom_data/pdf/annual-reports/ICMR_AR_English_2021_22_1.pdf) describes the same sortie. No new Bishnupur record is added: WHO refers to the Old DC Complex while ICMR identifies the district hospital for the same inaugural Karang route. These descriptions do not demonstrate two distinct dispatch sites.
