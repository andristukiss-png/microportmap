# Hamburg and Stockholm flight sites

Reviewed 3 November 2026. The CITYAM flight log and section 3 document two physical launch facilities. Medifly section 2 describes a separate hospital helipad used for dispatch. The earlier Hamburg university research cage and Stockholm research laboratory are separate facilities. Approximate points represent localities only.

- [dronePORT Hamburg](https://interreg-baltic.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Deliverable-2.2-Final.docx.pdf): The flight log covers several locations. Only the named dronePORT facility is counted here; the entry does not establish public access.
- [Farsta swimming hall rooftop drone dock](https://interreg-baltic.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Deliverable-2.2-Final.docx.pdf): The test established that the rooftop system could launch and recover the drone. It does not establish routine emergency response or surveillance operations.
- [Bundeswehr Hospital Hamburg Medifly launch site](https://edocs.tib.eu/files/e01fn21/1750939207.pdf): The receiving pathology department is not counted as a second sample-dispatch site. The trial is not evidence of a continuing medical transport service.
