# Twenty-sixth sourced expansion: university flight-test enclosures

Reviewed 30 October 2026. Two additions bring the catalogue to 329 research records across 40 countries, leaving 671 toward 1,000. All prior 327 records remain unchanged.

## Scope and evidence

- M-Air Net: Michigan Robotics explicitly identifies a netted outdoor facility used for research and teaching flights, with a staging pavilion and motion capture. It is one facility. Nearby university buildings, cameras and aircraft are not extra sites. The source does not establish a public landing or delivery service.
- PERCH: Penn's detailed GRASP facility statement identifies indoor UAV testing and an outdoor netted test area at the Pennovation Center. Both are counted within one PERCH campus record. The broader GRASP organisation operates at multiple locations; Levine Hall and other research groups are not silently included as additional flight sites. The source's indoor and outdoor test-area sections were read beyond the initial long page introduction.
- Both records use source-reported operation, with explicit limits on independent current verification. Publication dates remain null because the facility pages do not display clear publication dates. Map points are broad Ann Arbor and Philadelphia references, not source-derived flight-enclosure positions.

## Deferred candidates

- Colorado RECUV: the legacy infrastructure page describes an Indoor Flying Robot Lab, while the aerospace department describes a newer building completed in 2019. Resolve facility continuity before adding another Boulder record; fleet totals and COA areas are not ports.
- Cranfield: the current aerospace-autonomy-lab page confirms research capabilities but does not clearly identify a dedicated flight-test area. The initially guessed older URL returns a content-not-found page despite a successful HTTP status.
- CATEC: general UAS and robotics capabilities do not by themselves identify a separate Seville flight-test site. Existing ATLAS Alpha and Bravo records are not duplicated.
- Bristol: the old research paths redirect to broader university pages; those redirects are not facility evidence.
