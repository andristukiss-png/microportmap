# Gotō Islands medical dispatch sites

Reviewed 3 November 2026. The earlier ANA/Wingcopter record describes a 2021 route, not the Sora-iina/Zipline base completed in 2022. Tamanoura is a clinic launch; Aokata and Tomie are physical transfer points with documented onward takeoffs. Receiving rooftops, patients, drop zones without onward flight and additional routes were excluded. Coordinates are rounded locality references.

- [Sora-iina Fukue launch and recovery base](https://www.toyota-tsusho.com/english/about/project/25.html): Separate from the 2021 ANA / Wingcopter Fukue–Hisaka trial. Neither the number of routes nor the number of aircraft is counted as additional sites.
- [Tamanoura clinic prescription dispatch trial](https://www.toyota-tsusho.com/english/press/detail/250210_006531.html): The mobile clinic was the destination. The flight demonstrates a specific delivery, not continuing service from the clinic.
- [Aokata medical cargo relay site](https://www.pref.nagasaki.jp/doc/page-763935.html): One departure site served a hospital and the town hall. The church itself is not identified as the operator.
- [Tomie Port park medical cargo relay](https://www.acsl.co.jp/en/news-release/press-release/4573/): The park is identified in contemporaneous local reporting; ACSL documents the transfer and the receiving hospital. The hospital is not a second launch site.
