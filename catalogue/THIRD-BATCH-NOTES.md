The per-record source URLs, publication dates where supported, source-reading dates and notes are retained in `additions.json` and the built passports. Descriptions are short factual paraphrases. Source reading is not an operational inspection, and an attributed report is not independent verification of current operations.

Coordinates remain editorial locality references, not extracted facility positions or navigation coordinates. Some colocated regional references are intentionally shared; site identity comes from the named origin, store number, address or explicitly scoped programme rather than from pin uniqueness.

The source-type validator now accepts attributed original news reporting. It still rejects unknown source types, missing evidence, unsafe source URLs, duplicate IDs/entities, invalid coordinates and incomplete release manifests. No social posts, search-result counts, planned fleet totals or generic airport inventories are imported as sites.
