# Solent medical trial airfields

Reviewed 5 November 2026. Two historical airfield trial records bring the catalogue to 547 records across 53 countries.

The university report dated 13 May 2020 identifies the 9 May flight and mobile control centres at both airfields. Binstead qualifies through its return departure and control centre. No hospital destination is added. Current availability and access remain unverified.

Duplicate review covered all 545 prior records, including Thorney Island and Land’s End. Solent Airport / Lee-on-Solent is one site; Binstead / Binsted Farm is one site. Rounded airfield markers do not identify drone stands.
