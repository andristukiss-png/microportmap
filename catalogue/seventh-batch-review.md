# Seventh sourced expansion batch

Thirty additions bring the catalogue to 210 research records: 16 delivery origins or programmes and 14 explicitly labelled drone research/test facilities. These are not 210 verified operating microports. Prior 180 records remain unchanged.

## Counting and evidence review

- Manna Irish origins and Flytrex Holly Springs are historical following reported delivery pauses or cessation. Supporting links expose the status evidence alongside the original identity evidence. A pause is not a claim of permanent closure.
- Little Elm is conservatively announced because the cited Wing article schedules a future launch; the FAA source identifies the separate nest.
- Espoo is one area-scoped trial, not an invented exact base.
- Test centres are explicitly categorized separately from delivery infrastructure. ATLAS Alpha and Bravo have distinct documented locations. Multiple pads, hangars, customer campaigns, aircraft and range volumes are not additional sites. DronePort excludes the already-catalogued Citymesh SENSE dock.
- Coordinates are editorial locality references, not extracted facility coordinates. Undated sources retain null publication dates.
- The RTÉ Dublin location report was available through indexed text; direct access returned an error. The old Goondiwindi council URL was unavailable, so the readable Australian Aviation hub report is used instead.
- Additional sources are preserved in full JSON and shown on passports. CSV and GeoJSON retain their existing primary-source columns.
