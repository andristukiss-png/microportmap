# Twelfth sourced expansion: medical origin and test centres

Five additions reviewed on 29 October 2026 bring the catalogue to 265 research records across 38 countries. All previous 260 records remain unchanged. This batch adds one medical trial origin and four research/test campuses, not five independently verified delivery ports.

## Identity and status decisions

- Isala Meppel: the hospital launch report establishes the origin. LVNL’s August 2026 report confirms extension of the pilot into 2027. Status remains trial; Zwolle and the corridor are not separately counted.
- Cochstedt: DLR identifies its national UAS test centre. Campus facilities, later research buildings and individual programmes remain one record.
- Llanbedr: the operator’s dated release and CAA directory identify Snowdonia Aerospace Centre. Shared space-test and drone-test facilities are one campus.
- Predannack: WholeShip describes the National Drone Hub and the CAA directory supplies its location. The site’s runways, hangars and individual demonstrations are not additional ports.
- Cloncurry: the government’s December 2020 release confirms opening and initial flights. The later council report documents a Q1 2024–25 campaign but no further pipeline at that reporting point. The record therefore does not imply continuous availability.

All coordinates are editorial locality references, not facility or landing positions. A test-site listing is not public-access permission or aviation authorisation. CAA directory entries were reviewed directly; older downloadable fact sheets showed earlier validity dates, so no regulatory-validity claim is stored in the records.

## Proposals not added

The Cowes Mornington Road announcement was superseded by reporting that the council would not issue the site licence. The Morecambe Bay funding narrative did not establish completed hospital flights. Neither is added as an operating origin. The Baker Barracks chemotherapy campaign remains outside this batch pending a more focused trial-scope review.
