# Thirty-seventh sourced expansion: US university flight arenas

Reviewed 1 November 2026. Three additions bring the catalogue to 352 records across 41 countries; 648 remain toward 1,000. All prior 349 records remain unchanged.

## Source decisions

- [Johns Hopkins Robodrome](https://lcsr.jhu.edu/news/new-drone-arena-helps-scientists-design-aerial-robots-for-cities-and-spaceflight/), published 14 July 2026, describes drone experiments while completion and additional netting remained planned. Reported operation denotes research use in that report, not independently confirmed completion. Stieff Silver Building is distinct from the institutional footer address.
- [UT Knoxville ASL](https://volweb.utk.edu/~zwang124/res_facility.html) describes a netted campus flight arena. The [group homepage](https://volweb.utk.edu/~zwang124/index.html) supports institutional affiliation; its office contact is not assigned as the arena room. Publication date remains unknown.
- [Alabama departmental laboratories](https://aem.eng.ua.edu/research/) identifies ASCL and its dedicated arena. The [faculty profile](https://eng.ua.edu/eng-directory/dr-pan-zhao/) links to the [Controls and Autonomy Lab](https://cal.eng.ua.edu/). These names are not counted as two facilities. Publication date remains unknown.

Aircraft, partner institutions, other departmental labs and proposed applications do not create extra sites. Broad city coordinates are not building, launch or landing coordinates. Present operation and public access remain unverified.
