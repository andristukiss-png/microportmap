This is a source review, not a physical inspection or confirmation of present access.

Bibai Sky Park is a shared airfield, distinct from the existing agricultural surveying dock. Yoshino and Yamato are separate KDDI fields, distinct from the town-centre SkyHub depot. Takinoue's source describes trial use; Akebono's inconsistent indoor-area field is not used to infer building dimensions. Government listing of Technology Farm Nishinosato links to the Sapporo Dome PDF, so that mismatched lead is not added. Original publication dates remain null; event, reopening and privacy-policy dates are not substituted.
