Sources are linked per record. Operator announcements and issuer releases are attributed claims, not independent operational verification. The Ghana research paper is labelled `research`; equipment models remain unspecified where the cited source does not identify them. Dates are recorded only when supported; an undated listing is not assigned its search-engine crawl date as a publication date.

Amazon's updated College Station pharmacy page explicitly says the pilot ended and the site no longer fulfils orders. That record has `historical` status. ENAV's San Salvo report establishes a test, not a launched commercial network. The Grand Plaza, Zurich, Northridge and Silicon Valley records likewise retain trial scope where applicable.

Manual overlap review was performed against the 60 previously published records. Specific watch items for future imports are preserved in each `reviewNotes`: Pambegua versus the existing Kaduna State centre, Ogoja versus Cross River, Chemelil versus the Kisumu County proposal, and the earlier Wing/Apian versus later Matternet/Apian central London network. Future evidence may update those records rather than create duplicates.

All coordinates are editorial locality or regional references, not extracted facility coordinates. An addressed store and a town-centre point are different kinds of information; the record does not imply that the map pin identifies that store. Kaduna and Cross River remain state-level descriptions because the cited operator case study does not identify a specific town.

The build and tests now read the declared release count, rather than assuming a permanent 60-record ceiling. The first ten addition records are protected by explicit content digests. The new browser probes cover two distinct Katy origins sharing a locality, a Finnish rooftop, an ended pilot and an announced Kenyan hub, alongside the existing cross-catalogue journeys.
