# Helsinki health-related delivery trials

Reviewed 3 November 2026. Three separate launch locations for completed flights. These are distinct from the existing Vuosaari retail nest. Receiving sites, remote operators and the pharmacy supplying products are not counted as dispatch sites. Map points represent approximate localities only.

- [Lahnalahdenpuisto trial launch site](https://testbed.hel.fi/en/smart-mobility/various-delivery-pilot-projects-in-jatkasaari-helsinki-in-the-autumn/): No medications were carried. The pharmacy supplied the goods; the square was the collection point.
- [Kaivopuisto trial launch site](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/helsinki-fire-monitoring-rescue-services-and-delivery-emergency-medical-products-using-drones): The allergic emergency was scripted. The account does not establish treatment of an actual patient or routine service.
- [Kyläsaari Stara warehouse trial origin](https://interreg-baltic.eu/project-pilots/helsinki-pilot-2025/): Aviant supervised the flights remotely from Oslo with ground staff in Helsinki. The warehouse is the physical origin; the report describes a trial.
