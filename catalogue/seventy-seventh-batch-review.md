# Three hospital dispatch sites

Reviewed 4 November 2026. The Dominican Republic joins the catalogue. Each point is a locality reference; none establishes current operations.

- [Bohechío Hospital medical droneport](https://flyinglabs.org/assets/Use-cases/PDFs/Dominican-Republic-_Project-Use-Case_Delivering-essential-medicines-to-rural-health-facilities.pdf): The report explicitly identifies the hospital as the droneport. The two clinics are destinations of these deliveries, not additional origins in this batch.
- [Jengjal Sub-Divisional Hospital drone station](https://www.meghalaya.gov.in/sites/default/files/press_release/Meghalaya_Launched_its_First_Drone_Station_5th_Dec_2022.pdf): One dispatch station is counted. The network’s listed spokes do not each establish a separate origin; the published flight does not confirm the station’s present status.
- [Vikarabad District Hospital cargo trial](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/telangana-becomes-first-state-to-launch-drone-trials-for-deliveries-1028758.html): The parade ground hosted the ceremony. The article distinguishes it from the hospital departure point and does not establish routine deliveries.
