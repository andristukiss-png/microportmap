# Twentieth sourced expansion: US research flight facilities

Reviewed 30 October 2026. Five named facilities bring the catalogue to 305 records across 39 countries, leaving 695 toward 1,000. All prior 300 records remain unchanged. Research facilities are not represented as commercial delivery ports or independently verified current operations.

## Identity and evidence decisions

- Wallops: one airfield campus; runways, hangars, aircraft and repeated tests do not create extra records.
- OSU: the named Glencoe-area UAFS, supported by the university’s station location page, is one node. The wider Skyway network and proposed extensions are not counted. The office address in the Skyway page footer is not used as the flight station location.
- Maryland: UROC and the former UAS Test Site are one airport-based facility. The university separately identifies the Fearless Flight Facility in its Discovery District near College Park; these are two physical facilities, not two names for one site.
- Reno-Stead: one airport test facility, separate from Reno-Tahoe International Airport and from the statewide research programme. Airport ownership does not establish the operator of every research flight.
- Publication dates are null for undated facility pages. The explicitly dated UROC article retains 20 October 2022; the review date does not imply a new opening or independent activity check.
- All map points are broad editorial locality references, not source-extracted facility or landing coordinates. Published access information does not constitute permission to fly.
