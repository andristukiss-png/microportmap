# Thirty-ninth sourced expansion: Brest and Raleigh

Reviewed 1 November 2026. Three additions bring the catalogue to 358 records across 41 countries; 642 remain toward 1,000. All prior 355 records remain unchanged.

## Source decisions

- [ENSTA inauguration](https://www.ensta.fr/actualites/inauguration-du-bassin-dexperimentation-air-mer-unique-en-europe), published 24 June 2026, describes one combined basin and drone enclosure at Brest. The [platform description](https://www.ensta.fr/recherche-et-innovation/decouvrir-la-recherche/plateformes-experimentales/bassin-dexperimentation-air-mer) corroborates the facility. Do not split equipment or programmes into sites.
- [NC State AERIS facilities](https://research.csc.ncsu.edu/aerislab/about/) distinguishes the indoor flight arena from outdoor Lake Wheeler testing. The proposed additional cage is not counted or assumed completed. Publication date is unknown.
- [NC State AERPAW launch](https://news.ncsu.edu/2021/11/pawr-program-announces-launch-of-aerpaw-testbed-for-advanced-research-on-wireless-connectivity-and-unmanned-aerial-vehicles/), published 9 November 2021, supports Lake Wheeler infrastructure. The [deployment directory](https://aerpaw.org/deployment-areas/) identifies its drone experiments. One field site is counted across AERPAW and AERIS references. Towers, nodes, trailers and aircraft are not additional sites. Other platform locations are excluded from this record.

City points are approximate research references, not facility boundaries or landing coordinates. Current operations, access and permissions remain independently unverified. SDU at HCA Airport was deferred because its relationship to the existing Odense test-centre record needs further counting review.
