The review pool consists of 24 Drone Juku school listings, JULC Kasukabe plus its first 21 regional listings, and four JUAVAC follow-up venues. One resolved physical venue is accepted per school candidate; additional named sites remain follow-up leads. No office address is substituted for a flight ground.

Rooftop sports courts and public gyms are shared training venues, not exclusive drone infrastructure. Indoor and outdoor areas on a single campus are grouped. The former Usuda school is one campus-level record: its two gyms are listed by the municipality, but the operator does not specify the individual hall. Saku's truck training hall is theory-only. The Yasu youth centre is distinct from ASKHIM's Chikuzen field; generic testimonials about a former school are not used as evidence of its building type.

The Sendai Nagamachi, Shizuoka Nakajima, Toyohashi industrial training centre, Airstage Obihiro and Sekido Yokohama venues already have records. JULC Niigata's Sanwa Seibu Sports House needs overlap resolution against the address-withheld DearStaff field before addition. The Sanwa No address is not substituted for that field. JULC Gunma practical training is identified at the former Kambai School in Midori rather than the Maebashi office. Its course page schedules December 2026 training and describes existing facilities; it does not establish that the future course has occurred.

Follow-up leads: Rosa's separate LABI rooftop; Koriyama Tamura Community Centre; AOBA Sports Park; Imizu UPD field; Minamiechizen Sakai and Kaerushika gyms; Cerezo Futsal Park; Toon and Tobe venues; JULC Mie's other named practical venues; JULC regional campuses west of Mie. Markers remain rounded editorial locality references, not launch coordinates. Current operation, access and flight permission are not independently verified. Undated descriptions and course listings do not guarantee current availability.
