# Fifteenth sourced expansion: Shenzhen commercial hubs

Five origins reviewed on 29 October 2026 bring the catalogue to 280 records across 39 countries. All prior 275 records remain unchanged. Four Meituan delivery bases and one Fengyi parcel hub are added.

## Identity and status

- Yitian Holiday Plaza: the operator-hosted Guangming Daily report identifies the departure point. The April 2023 publication date is taken from the displayed article date, not the later identifier in its URL. It is distinct from Xili Yitian Holiday Li.
- Haiya Mega Mall: the December 2023 report explicitly names the launch point. COFCO is the destination; later merchants and routes do not create new origins. Government coverage in October 2024 also records the route.
- Baoneng: the source identifies the Nanshan commercial-area airport. This is not the Luohu mall with a similar name. The article’s ambiguous enumeration of receiving points is not used as a facility count.
- Galaxy WORLD: the April 2025 district report describes a visit and observed deliveries. The August 2024 Longgang source identifies the Bantian site. Bao’an is the publishing district of the later report, not the facility district. The wider park, COCO Park and Meituan base are counted once; the separate Futian mall is excluded.
- Fengyi Shiyan: the government report identifies operational takeoffs and landings. June 2025 reporting distinguishes Shiyan from the Xin’an centre. Neither connected outlets nor aircraft totals become extra records.

All retain source-reported operation, with current operation independently unverified. Later-status searches did not establish uninterrupted service. Coordinates are editorial locality references, not exact facility positions. No fleet-wide aircraft model is assigned to a site. The existing Futian police dock network is a different operator and purpose.
