# English medical flight trial origins

Reviewed 5 November 2026. Three historical trial sites bring the catalogue to 543 records across 53 countries.

Baker Barracks is a named prescription-delivery origin. Land’s End Airport and West Wellow are documented research locations. Each counts once, with payload testing distinguished from patient delivery. The university page has no displayed publication date, so both research records retain null publication dates.

Duplicate review covered all 540 previous records. Other experiments at Baker Barracks do not create additional sites. St Mary’s Hospital and Isles of Scilly destinations are excluded without separate origin evidence. West Wellow is explicitly locality-scoped because a permanent facility is not named.
