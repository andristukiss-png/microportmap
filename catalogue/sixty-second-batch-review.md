# Nepal: DrOTS hubs and specimen return posts

Reviewed 2 November 2026. Sections 2.7 and 4.1–4.3 and Annex 3 of the [project report](https://we-robotics.files.svdcdn.com/production/assets/Documents-Reports-PDF/DroTS-Report.pdf) distinguish 12 posts initially mapped from the eight operating routes. Two testing hubs dispatched the aircraft; staff at the eight networked posts loaded specimens for return. No record is added for the four other mapped posts. The cover does not establish a publication day, so dates in the passports refer to flights, not publication. The totals in different report summaries do not match exactly and are not used as site counts.
