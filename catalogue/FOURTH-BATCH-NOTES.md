## Counting decisions

- Origins, shared networks and testing programmes count once. Recipients, route counts, aircraft and unnamed planned stations do not increase the total.
- Shintotsukawa remains a trial entry because this report confirms a depot ceremony and demonstration but schedules paid services for later.
- Tsuruga describes a completed two-day temporary-depot campaign. Future permanent-depot evidence needs overlap review.
- Inhambane is marked historical because the implementation partner marks the study complete. Its two unnamed hubs count as one study network.
- Abu Dhabi is one announced network; forty proposed ports are not forty records. Other medical drone programmes there need overlap review.
- Publication dates are left null where not established. Every coordinate is an editorial locality reference, never a facility location extracted from these sources.
