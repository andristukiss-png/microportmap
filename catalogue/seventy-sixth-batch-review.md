# Njala runway and two Amazon trial origins

Reviewed 4 November 2026. Sierra Leone joins the catalogue. All three are historical research references with approximate map points.

- [Njala humanitarian drone test runway](https://www.unicef.org/wca/press-releases/drones-good-corridor-launched-drones-take-flight-deliver-medicine-remote-areas): The runway is counted once. UNICEF describes a capability demonstration; this does not establish regular medical delivery or public access.
- [Contamana football field medical cargo trial](https://werobotics.org/assets/blog/2017/02/WeRobotics-Amazon-Rainforest-Cargo-Drones-Report.pdf): The football field was the outbound launch site, rather than the health centre supplying the trial. The exercise did not document treatment of an actual snakebite patient.
- [Pampa Hermosa blood sample return trial](https://werobotics.org/assets/blog/2017/02/WeRobotics-Amazon-Rainforest-Cargo-Drones-Report.pdf): Pampa Hermosa counts because a new payload departed from it. The aircraft overshot the intended field and landed in a tree; the report says the sample was recovered intact.
