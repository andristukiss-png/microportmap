# Thirty-eighth sourced expansion: European university flight arenas

Reviewed 1 November 2026. Three additions bring the catalogue to 355 records across 41 countries; 645 remain toward 1,000. All prior 352 records remain unchanged.

## Source decisions

- [ENAC Toulouse Occitanie](https://www.enac.fr/en/drone-flight-arena-toulouse-occitanie-0) describes one instrumented arena and associated facilities. The [DRONES platform](https://drones.recherche.enac.fr/) supports institutional context. Do not count other campuses from the footer, workshops, simulated traffic or individual aircraft as additional sites.
- [EPFL LIS recruitment page](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/lis/open-positions/) describes an existing instrumented flight arena. The [laboratory homepage](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/lis/) supports Lausanne context. Its office contact is not assigned as the arena room, and an undated position listing does not prove current recruitment.
- [TUM Flying Systems Lab](https://www.mirmi.tum.de/en/mirmi/research/labs/flying-systems-lab/) identifies Garching, supported by the [winter 2023/24 teaching page](https://srl.cit.tum.de/teaching/w23/rmilpracticals). Lab and arena naming variants at the same address are counted once. The separate Munich teaching venue is not added as a flight arena.

All three primary pages are undated; publication dates remain unknown. Approximate city points are not building, launch or landing coordinates. Present operation and public access remain unverified. Luxembourg’s AeRoLab page redirected to maintenance and was not included. Imperial’s facilities page could not be opened and was not included.

## Verification scope

Canonical hashes preserve all 302 previous additions; existing snapshots preserve the original 50 records and 72 HTML pages. Browser coverage includes 360 routes at two sizes (720 journeys). Checks cover exports, sources, identities and deterministic builds.
