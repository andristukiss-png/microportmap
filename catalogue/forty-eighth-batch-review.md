# Forty-eighth sourced expansion: Boston, Scaife Hall and Tulsa

Reviewed 1 November 2026. Three additions bring the catalogue to 385 records across 42 countries; 615 remain toward 1,000. All prior 382 records remain unchanged.

## Source decisions

- [Northeastern report](https://news.northeastern.edu/2025/08/29/faculty-staff/how-to-fly-drones-beginner-tips/), published 29 August 2025: identifies the cage in the Boston EXP robotics bay. The [equipment page](https://robotics.northeastern.edu/our-equipment/) lists motion capture and the [contact page](https://robotics.northeastern.edu/contact/) locates EXP in Boston. This is not the Burlington ECUAS facility. Other equipment and general flying advice do not create sites or permissions.
- [CMU Scaife Hall description](https://www.cmu.edu/cdfd/buildings/scaife/index.html), undated: reports a completed building with a high-bay drone arena. The [engineering directory](https://engineering.cmu.edu/research/facilities.html) also lists it. Scaife in Oakland is distinct from Hazelwood RIC. Building size, eleven other labs and the 2023 building completion are not arena capacity, extra drone sites or page publication date. The directory’s archived detail link returned a not-found page; the accessible campus-building description is used as primary source.
- [ORU Tandy Labs expansion report](https://oru.edu/news/oru_news/2024/oru-celebrates-ribbon-cutting-tandy-lab.php), dated 8 November 2024: describes an added drone arena and control room. These comprise one facility. The wider grant and non-drone laboratories are not a drone-only budget or extra ports; this is distinct from existing Tulsa delivery and droneport records.

All points are broad editorial locality references. Current operations, access and flight permissions remain independently unverified.
