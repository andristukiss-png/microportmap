# Scottish airport postal trials

Reviewed 5 November 2026. Two historical dispatch origins bring the catalogue to 540 records across 53 countries.

- Kirkwall Airport is identified by its operator as the mainland origin of the October 2021 North Ronaldsay postal trial. It is distinct from the existing Stromness hub and its Kirkwall road-transfer point. Aircraft capacity and intended return transport do not establish another dispatch site.
- Tingwall Airport is named in Royal Mail's retrospective of its Shetland trial. A local report dated 1 April 2022 independently identifies the departure airport and Windracers. The retrospective has no displayed publication date, so that field remains null. The record uses the Shetland locality, not the similarly named Orkney village.

Both are historical trial records. Current service, exact landing locations and public access remain unverified. Duplicate review covered all 538 prior records. No separate Unst or North Ronaldsay record is added on the evidence reviewed.
