# St Merryn flight testing

Reviewed 5 November 2026. One historical flight-test site brings the catalogue to 550 records across 53 countries.

Business Cornwall's dated 20 May 2020 report documents demonstrations of ULTRA upgrades at the named airfield. The Cornwall Space Cluster brochure identifies November 2019 tests with Southampton and Windracers. The news publication date and flight timing remain separate. The brochure's file-path year is not treated as a publication date, and present access is not inferred from promotional wording.

Duplicate review covered all 549 previous records and airfield aliases. St Merryn is distinct from Perranporth and Land’s End. Multiple flights count as one site. The rounded marker is an airfield reference, not a drone stand.
