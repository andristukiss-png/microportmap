# Mongolia: two dispatch origins in Ulaanbaatar

Reviewed 4 November 2026. Both use the same city-level reference point; neither source is a landing-site survey.

- [Ulaanbaatar National Center for Transfusion Medicine](https://aeronext.com/news/422-drone-deliveries-in-mongolia/): One named dispatch centre is counted. Established routes do not demonstrate a launch site at each hospital. The 2023 trial from this centre is earlier evidence for the same origin.
- [Ulaanbaatar City Hall branch office food delivery trial](https://aeronext.com/news/mongolia_fooddelivery/): The branch office lot was the reported launch site; the restaurant supplied food and the Summer House received it. The source does not give a street address for the lot.

The [postal operator](https://epostne4.mongolpost.mn/post/5181) confirms a branch-to-branch flight but does not identify its dispatch branch. The postal trial adds no site in this release.
