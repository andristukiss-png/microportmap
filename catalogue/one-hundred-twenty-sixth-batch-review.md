The pool comprises the remaining 18 JMA regional listings, all 23 DDA affiliated-school listings, and nine named follow-ups. Each accepted candidate contributes one physical campus. A school office or membership listing alone is not evidence of a flight venue.

Kibichuo and Toon Twin Dome are already represented. JMA Osaka practical instruction is in Yamato-Takada, Nara; IDS Ikegami uses Nakago Arena in Kisarazu; JMA Fukuoka South uses the former Mimata Elementary School in Okawa. These records use the practical-site locality rather than the office city.

Indoor and outdoor areas at a single campus are grouped. CHRES training is supported by an operator notice dated 16 April 2023 and its venue directory. Historical evidence does not guarantee present availability. Hofu has differing address forms on the same page, so no precise facility coordinate is asserted.

Resolve the former Tamatani School relationship to the existing municipal gym. Aso-region addresses still lack clear practical roles; recent operator news names Yagogawa Community Centre as a separate lead.

Markers are rounded editorial locality references, never launch coordinates. Current operation, public access and flight permissions are not independently verified.
