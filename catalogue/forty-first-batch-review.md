Reviewed 1 November 2026. Three additions bring the catalogue to 364 records across 41 countries; 636 remain toward 1,000. All prior 361 records remain unchanged.

## Source decisions

- [UC Digital Futures research](https://www.uc.edu/news/articles/2026/10/uc-drones-sound-damage-can-tell-if-damaged-prevent-crash.html), published 29 October 2026: one laboratory cage, distinct from the existing police DFR record. Mock warehouse experiments and proposed docking applications are not operating commercial sites.
- [UC San Diego Aerodrome opening](https://jacobsschool.ucsd.edu/news/release/2460?id=2460), published 25 January 2018: the opened outdoor enclosure is counted once; the proposed indoor expansion is not assumed completed. The [facility page](https://ucsd-aerodrome.github.io/) describes later instrumentation, not a second site. Construction, opening and equipment dates differ.
- [Northeastern Burlington opening](https://news.northeastern.edu/2019/04/08/northeastern-university-in-partnership-with-the-navy-and-air-force-opens-state-of-the-art-drone-testing-facility-on-its-burlington-campus/), published 8 April 2019: one connected indoor/outdoor research facility. Chambers, corridors, partners and institutional naming variants do not increase the site count. The main lab and directory pages returned gateway errors; the dated university opening report was accessible and is the primary source. Differing directory street numbers are not used to place a precise map point.

City points are approximate research references, not facility boundaries or landing coordinates. Current operations, access and permissions remain independently unverified.
