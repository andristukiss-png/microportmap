# Twenty-fifth sourced expansion: Marknesse and Payerne test environments

Reviewed 30 October 2026. Two additions bring the catalogue to 327 research records across 40 countries, leaving 673 toward 1,000. All prior 325 records remain unchanged.

## Identity, status and evidence

- Marknesse: NLR's facility page explicitly identifies the Drone Centre and its UAV flight-test infrastructure. DigiCity is an addition within this same centre, not another campus. The 2025 QRDF launch article describes use of existing NLR infrastructure, so the initiative does not generate a second site record. The facility page says 16 DigiCity containers while the 2023 article says 24; neither number is promoted into a current verified inventory. Aircraft counts and airspace dimensions are not site counts or public flying permission.
- Payerne: the public drone white-paper landing page explicitly describes support for drone manufacturers, operators and research teams at Payerne Airport. The flight-test service page corroborates the offering and preparation/approval process; the homepage clarifies swiss aeropole's civil airport responsibilities. Only these public pages were read. The email-gated PDF was not requested or accessed. The 250-plus cumulative test-flight figure describes broader aviation experience; it is not represented as 250 drone flights or sites.
- Both records use source-reported operation, not independent current verification. Principal pages do not display clear publication dates; these remain null. Dated supporting NLR articles retain their own dates, not dates substituted for the principal pages. Coordinates are broad editorial references to Marknesse and Payerne, not source-derived campus or runway positions.

## Deferred candidates

- Helsinki-East/Pyhtää: the reviewed Redstone pages describe airport development and future unmanned aviation; stronger evidence of a specific drone-testing operation is still needed for this batch.
- Västervik and Nummela: relevant official sources were inaccessible or unresolved; no record was inferred from a remembered name.
- Texas A&M-Corpus Christi ARI: its FAQ identifies an 11-range programme. The office address is not assumed to be a launch site, and the range total is not converted into individual facilities. ARI and former Lone Star UAS Center are the same institutional lineage for future duplicate review.
