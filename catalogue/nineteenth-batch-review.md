# Nineteenth sourced expansion: historical Texas retail origins

Reviewed 30 October 2026. Six additions bring the catalogue to 300 records across 39 countries, leaving 700 toward 1,000. All 294 previous records remain unchanged. This milestone counts research records, including historical sites, not 300 currently operating microports.

## Evidence and identity decisions

Walmart’s dated release establishes the former delivery origins. Later WHRO/VCIJ reporting establishes the end of the partnership, so all six entries are historical. Current physical infrastructure, replacement operators and exact closure dates are not established.

The FAA-hosted file is titled Draft Environmental Assessment, August 2024, despite its filename ending in Final. Its Table 1-1 supports address cross-checking only. Proposed Part 135 expansion, aircraft models and permitted flight volumes are not used as evidence of later implementation. Map points remain independently chosen broad locality references rather than copied aviation coordinates.

- Richardson: Buckingham Road is separate from Amazon’s Research Drive facility.
- Mesquite: Town East is separate from the existing Zipline Belt Line Road/US 80 origin.
- Dallas Buckner and Gus Thomasson: separate street addresses from each other and the existing Samuell/Forest Lane sites.
- Gus Thomasson: the FAA draft uses Mesquite, while Walmart uses Dallas. The same address is counted once, retaining Walmart’s locality label.
- The Colony: the retailer directory confirms store 426. Its retail opening status is not evidence of continued drone operations.
- Rowlett is deferred because the existing city-network record requires further facility-level reconciliation before another origin can be counted.
