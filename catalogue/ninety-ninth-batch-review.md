# Waterford Airport U-Flyte trials

Reviewed 7 November 2026. One record brings the catalogue to 568 records across 53 countries.

Maynooth University’s 21 September 2018 report explicitly names Waterford Airport as U-Flyte’s primary flight-testing base and documents completed trials. The Irish Aviation Authority published a supporting report on 28 September 2018. These publication dates are not treated as exact flight dates. The record remains historical without inferring current operations or an emergency-response service.

Duplicate review covered all 567 existing records and aliases Waterford, EIWF, U-Flyte and Maynooth. This is one airport-based research site: five tests, flight areas, university premises and participating organisations are not additional records. The map marker is a broad Waterford locality reference, not an operational coordinate. Current access and test bookings remain unverified.
