Thirty additions reviewed on 28 October 2026 bring the catalogue to 180 research records: 17 retail origins and 13 dock-based public-safety or industrial programmes. All prior 150 records remain unchanged. Canonical JSON digests preserve the 100 previously added records; the seed checks preserve the original 50.

## Counting and limitations

- The nine Charlotte-region Walmart addresses are seven reported origins and two forthcoming origins in the October 2026 WBTV report. Kannapolis retail is distinct from the existing Zipline medical record. Charlotte Arboretum and Walmart Galleria are separate origins.
- The FAA assessment distinguishes Frisco Eldorado from the already listed Preston Road and Frisco Station nests. The planned Stonebrook site remains unchanged.
- The two North Richland Hills Walmart nests are separate from the newly approved BISD DoorDash origin. City zoning approval is not proof of service commencement.
- Dallas Samuell is reported as launched in the city memo. Forest Lane is only scheduled; passage of its target date does not establish implementation.
- Each public-safety programme is counted once. Aircraft, docks at unnamed roofs, incidents, patrol vehicles and later rollout phases do not increase the record count. Skydio and Axon case studies are supplier accounts, not independent verification. Equipment drawn only from case-study tags is explicitly labelled.
- Harford's synopsis reports a launch but its officer transcript uses future tense. It is conservatively marked announced pending clearer implementation evidence.
- LNG Canada is one facility deployment, distinct from Vancouver Police. The facility's Kitimat locality is corroborated by the operator.
- Coordinates are editorial city/locality references, not facility positions, even where a source contains precise coordinates. Undated case studies keep null publication dates. Event dates and publication dates are not interchanged.
