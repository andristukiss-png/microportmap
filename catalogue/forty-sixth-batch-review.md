# Forty-sixth sourced expansion: Toronto, Brin and Stock Pavilion

Reviewed 1 November 2026. Three additions bring the catalogue to 379 records across 41 countries; 621 remain toward 1,000. All prior 376 records remain unchanged.

## Source decisions

- [Toronto teaching report](https://www.utias.utoronto.ca/news/flying-through-obstacles-the-gravity-defying-course-on-unmanned-aerial-systems/), published 8 June 2023: documents flight tests in the Myhal arena. The [building description](https://www.engineering.utoronto.ca/myhal-centre-for-engineering-innovation-entrepreneurship/) identifies the level-five drone space as the Norris Walker 5T7 Robotics Laboratory. One facility; the Dufferin Street footer is not its location.
- [Maryland Brin lab](https://facilities.robotics.umd.edu/dronelab/home.html), undated: Robotics Drone Lab is an alias. The [contact page](https://facilities.robotics.umd.edu/dronelab/contact.html) separates the lab from its manager’s office. The [facilities directory](https://robotics.umd.edu/facilities) lists the indoor lab separately from F3 and UROC, both already catalogued.
- [Illinois facilities](https://autonomy.illinois.edu/facilities), undated: Stock Pavilion is distinct from the smaller Autonomy Arena added previously. The [director’s message](https://autonomy.illinois.edu/education/meng/director-message) confirms aerial robotics testing; space and tracking-system dimensions are not assumed interchangeable. The [2024 exhibition venue](https://digitalag.illinois.edu/2024-safer-ag-workshop/i-farm-saferag-exhibition/) establishes Urbana context, not a publication date or multiple permanent drone sites.

All points are broad editorial locality references. Current operations, access and flight permissions remain independently unverified.
