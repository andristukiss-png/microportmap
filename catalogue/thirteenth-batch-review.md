# Thirteenth sourced expansion: aerospace research facilities

Five additions reviewed on 29 October 2026 bring the catalogue to 270 research records across 39 countries. New Zealand is newly represented. All previous 265 records remain unchanged. These additions describe research/testing infrastructure, not verified consumer-delivery ports.

## Counting and status

- Tāwhaki: the operator confirms opening at Kaitorete and prior uncrewed test activity. Runway construction, planned hangars and customer flights are phases or uses of the same campus. The map deliberately uses nearby Birdlings Flat as a locality reference.
- Choctaw Nation: the June 2025 opening report identifies the Daisy facility. Its later FAA designation and Daisy Ranch name do not create another site.
- Nenana: UAF reports October 2025 flights at its airport hangar, following the 2024 hangar announcement. The base is separate from Alaska DOT’s existing SOAR dock programme. Operational research infrastructure does not imply a scheduled cargo service.
- Virginia Tech: the university’s facilities page separately identifies the Kentland laboratory and main-campus netted Drone Park. Its engineering article explicitly contrasts the two locations. They are two facilities, not two programmes within one campus. Both use the same editorial Blacksburg point; neither is a surveyed facility coordinate.

All five records use source-reported operation status for the facility itself. Current operation, public access and flight permission remain unverified. No test campaign, aircraft, hangar or delivery endpoint is counted as an additional port.
