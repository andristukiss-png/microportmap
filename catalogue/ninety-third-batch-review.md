# Draycott and Goodwood flight sites

Reviewed 6 November 2026. Two records bring the catalogue to 559 records across 53 countries.

Draycott: Southampton reports a successful student UAV flight near Swindon. Its publication date, 7 January 2009, is retained without inventing the exact flight date or a permanent drone facility.

Goodwood: the operator describes a drone development and testing centre at the aerodrome, jointly run by Goodwood and Across Safety Development. The operator pages have maintenance timestamps but no established original publication date. The March 2021 Catapult announcement independently identifies the centre as a planned trial host; this announcement is not treated as proof of completed trials.

Duplicate review covered all 557 prior records, including Draycott/Draycot, Goodwood and Chichester. Draycott is separate from Swindon DroneTEX and Wroughton. Goodwood's hangars, services and trial projects count as one centre. Both map points are broad locality references, not airfield or takeoff coordinates. Current operations and access remain unverified.
