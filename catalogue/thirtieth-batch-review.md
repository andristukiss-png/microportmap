# Thirtieth sourced expansion: two Bristol research sites

Reviewed 31 October 2026. Two additions bring the catalogue to 335 records across 40 countries; 665 remain toward 1,000. All previous 333 records are unchanged.

## Evidence and distinct identities

The [university aerospace facilities page](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/research/centres/bristol-aerospace-research-centre/facilities/) separately identifies an indoor BRL flight arena and outdoor drone testing at Fenswood. [BRL's own site](https://www.bristolroboticslab.com/) establishes its joint university partnership and Frenchay location. The [aerial robotics course](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/study/postgraduate/taught/msc-aerial-robotics/) supports use of both indoor and outdoor flight facilities.

The [Fenswood Farm page](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/gardens/research/fenswood-farm/) establishes Long Ashton locality and drone/automated-flight research. The older research station's closure is not evidence that current farm research has ended. Land-area totals differ between university pages; no area, flight boundary or airspace permission is inferred. Farms, rooms, courses and aircraft are not multiplied into sites.

Primary pages are undated, so publication dates remain null. Copyright years and future course starts are not publication dates. Both map points are broad editorial locality references, not geocoded facility positions. Reported-operation reflects university descriptions, not independently inspected present activity.

## Discovery and exclusions

Earlier Bristol candidates were deferred because the generic robotics overview did not establish a flight arena. The now-reviewed aerospace facilities page provides that specific evidence. The older Flight Lab facilities URL returned a fetch error and is not used as a record source. Cranfield DART remains unadded without usable facility evidence.

## Verification

Canonical hashes preserve all 283 previous additions; snapshots preserve the original 50 records and 72 HTML pages. Automatic browser coverage expands to 340 routes at two sizes (680 journeys), including both distinct Bristol passports. Catalogue checks cover exports, source safety and deterministic build output.
