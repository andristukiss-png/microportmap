# Forty-ninth sourced expansion: Reno, Hayward and Sherbrooke

Reviewed 1 November 2026. Three additions bring the catalogue to 388 records across 42 countries; 612 remain toward 1,000. All prior 385 records remain unchanged.

## Source decisions

- [NCAR infrastructure](https://www.unr.edu/ncar/infrastructure) describes the main-campus high-bay drone arena, with tracking and a viewing balcony. The [Applied Research Facility directory](https://www.unr.edu/ncar/infrastructure/arf) supports the building identity. This is separate from the existing Reno-Stead airport facility. Neither page establishes a publication date or a precise landing point.
- [East Bay Computer Engineering report](https://www.csueastbay.edu/csci/files/docs/assessment/semesters/annual-reports/2024-2025-annual-reports/bscmpe-capr_2024-2025-final.pdf), page 4, describes a drone lab and says the original cage closed before a shared Braddock lab opened. One replacement facility is counted, with new cage layout and room left unknown. The [Hayward campus map](https://www.csueastbay.edu/about/files/docs/hayward-hills-campus-map.pdf) establishes the building locality. The submission date is 13 October 2025, not an asserted publication date. Academic-year labels conflict; neither those labels nor the filename establishes an opening year. VBT 230 electronics-lab plans are excluded.
- [Sherbrooke robotics facilities](https://www.usherbrooke.ca/3it/en/technological-ecosystem/robotics) describes an indoor drone flight room with motion capture. The [3IT contact page](https://www.usherbrooke.ca/3it/en/contact-us) places the institute in Sherbrooke. The flight room counts once; observation, telehealth and recording rooms are not extra sites. The facility page is undated. Its older contact link returned 404; the working institute contact page is used instead.

All map points are broad editorial locality references. Current operation, access and flight permissions remain independently unverified. No publication dates are invented from submission dates, copyright dates or search crawl timestamps.
