# Fourteenth sourced expansion: Meituan delivery origins

Five named origins reviewed on 29 October 2026 bring the catalogue to 275 research records across 39 countries. All previous 270 records remain unchanged. These are source-reported delivery facilities, not independently verified operating ports.

## Identity and status review

- Coastal City: operator evidence establishes a regular Talent Park route in September 2023. The June 2024 Bay Park pilot is another route associated with this commercial area, not another origin. The latter source supplies an update date, not a new publication date for the main record.
- UpperHills: operator evidence names the origin in April 2024; February 2026 reporting describes activity at the takeoff point. Restaurant launches and park destinations are not additional facilities.
- Longhua Tianhong: a 2023 report identifies the service origin. An April 2024 site visit locates the launch point in Fendouzhe Square across the street from the mall. September 2026 network upgrades concern receiving nodes; their M-Port 3 equipment is not attributed to this origin. Tianhong and the square are one record, not two.
- Bailian Jinshan: the Shanghai authority identifies a commercial route origin in the shopping mall, launched in April 2023. The separate UAV test base and business-association receiving building are excluded.
- Hopson One: the December 2023 report explicitly identifies mall takeoff; the transport authority records a February 2025 site visit and demonstration. The management centre and additional routes are not extra origins.

All five retain dated source-reported-operation status with explicit current-operation limitations. Targeted later-status searches do not independently establish uninterrupted service. No model is inferred from fleet-wide announcements. All map points are editorial locality approximations, not source-derived launch coordinates. The original Shenzhen police dock network is a different operator and purpose.
