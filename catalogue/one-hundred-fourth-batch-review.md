# Plymouth and Essex aerial robotics facilities

Reviewed 8 November 2026. Two records bring the catalogue to 574 records across 53 countries.

Plymouth explicitly offers drone-aircraft testing in its Autonomous Systems Test Laboratory in the Babbage Building. Essex describes its Robot Arena as accommodating flying robots. Each named physical facility counts once; equipment, connected rooms and research projects do not create additional records.

Both university descriptions are undated, so publication and opening dates are not inferred. Duplicate review covered all 572 existing records and Plymouth/Babbage and Essex/Colchester/Robot Arena aliases, with no existing entry found. Map markers are broad locality references. Current operation, bookings and access remain unverified.
