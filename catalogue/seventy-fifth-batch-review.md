# Medical logistics in Bhutan, Madagascar and Vanuatu

Reviewed 4 November 2026. Bhutan and Madagascar are new country entries. These are historical records; location markers represent localities only.

- [Thimphu national referral hospital quadcopter trial](https://bhutannewsnetwork.com/2014/08/silicon-valley-chooses-bhutan-for-drones-tests/): The record describes a field trial. The number of completed flights and routine delivery of patient supplies are not established; the three unnamed units are not counted.
- [Maroantsetra medical supply dispatch base](https://ghevals.meandahq.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/GHEVALS_009_UAV_PILOT_REPORT_211018_508.pdf): The USAID and Global Fund activities shared a takeoff point. Neither the separate stock warehouse nor the receiving communes are counted here.
- [Melsisi vaccine distribution and launch base](https://www.unicef.org/innovation/media/11956/file/Reference%203.8.%20Project%20Final%20Report_WINGCOPTER.pdf): The final report identifies Melsisi as the origin of all flights in this deployment. Its vaccine drop-off locations are not additional launch bases.
