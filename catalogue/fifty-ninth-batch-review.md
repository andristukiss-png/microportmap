# India: medical drone programmes and documented dispatch sites

Reviewed 2 November 2026. The [29 October 2024 ministry release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2069266&lang=2&reg=48) lists 11 distinct institutions. Each receives one programme record, not a claim of a dedicated pad or completed flight. The Rishikesh helicopter service is excluded.

Two further records describe the January 2024 [Bhubaneswar–Tangi flight](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1999705&lang=2&reg=48): blood outbound, samples on return. The 2021 [Bishnupur dispatch](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1760806&lang=2&reg=48) is separate from Imphal. Karang is a delivery destination, not a separately counted origin. All locations are approximate and current service is unverified.
