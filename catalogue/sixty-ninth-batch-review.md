# Japanese cargo and relief dispatch sites

Reviewed 3 November 2026. Ten distinct origins were compared against the 490 preceding records. Three supplied communities affected by disasters; seven were temporary trials or exercises. Shared destinations, planned pads, remote pilots, supplying shops and onward rail legs do not count. The two Matsue entries share a city-level marker but have individually identified physical origins.

- [Hachijō Plaza Park delivery trial](https://aeronext.co.jp/news/hachijotown_poc/): The reported flight total includes tests. Neither the supplying shops nor the recipients are counted as launch sites.
- [Iwate Mori no Arena relief exercise](https://aeronext.co.jp/news/iwatetown_poc/): The medicine package was an empty demonstration box. This was an exercise rather than an emergency deployment.
- [Hioki community hall shopping trial](https://aeronext.co.jp/achievements/kagoshima/): The marker represents Hioki; the supermarket was a supplier, not the documented launch site.
- [Yamagawa Port market freight trial](https://aeronext.co.jp/achievements/kagoshima/): The source distinguishes fish flown by drone from an oversized fish sent by road. The route to Hakata was not a drone flight.
- [Suetsugu Park document delivery trial](https://aeronext.co.jp/news/matsue-drone-poc/): This entry covers the park origin of the document phase, not the separate prepared-meal flights.
- [Morzwell prepared-meal dispatch trial](https://aeronext.co.jp/news/matsue-drone-poc/): The two delivery routes share one dispatch base. The city-level point does not locate a particular facility entrance.
- [Nakatsugawa landslide relief launch site](https://aeronext.co.jp/achievements/andproject/): The project describes the site relative to the landslide. The Tokyo remote-control location and road collection point are not additional flight origins.
- [Wajima City Cultural Hall earthquake relief dispatch](https://aeronext.co.jp/achievements/wajima/): This relief flight was separate from the September flood response and from the prefecture’s inspection dock programme.
- [Unyū fishing port flood relief dispatch](https://aeronext.co.jp/news/noto_transportation_drone/): The aircraft were brought from other depots. This record counts only the local departure point for the relief flight.
- [Former Tenryū Junior High School relief exercise](https://aeronext.co.jp/news/tenryu-drone-workshop/): Contemporaneous reporting identifies the direction of flight. The wider list of candidate landing sites was a plan, not a set of completed launches.
