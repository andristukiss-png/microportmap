# Fifth fifty candidate-site screening tasks

Reviewed 8 November 2026. Completed 50 further candidate screenings: 20 accepted records, 19 deferred candidates and 11 overlaps excluded. The catalogue reaches 660 research records across 53 countries, leaving 340 to the target of 1,000.

Each task screens one candidate for evidence and distinct physical scope. A completed screening can defer addition. The machine-readable ledger is [2026-11-08-fifth-fifty-task-review.json](2026-11-08-fifth-fifty-task-review.json). Jeonju is a re-review of an earlier deferred lead after the updated directory supplied a direct operator link; the physical flight-site scope remains unresolved.

Accepted records describe reported test, training or designated flying infrastructure. Dronolandia is explicitly a recreational drone course. These are research records, not a count of verified operating commercial ports. Current operation, access, bookings and flight permission are not independently verified. Locality markers are editorial approximations, not surveyed launch coordinates. Undated descriptions retain null publication dates; authorization dates, founding years and PDF directory dates are not substituted.

Counting review covered all 640 existing records and aliases. Česká Lípa is separate from CTU’s Prague arena; its primary university page was indexed, but direct retrieval timed out. The malformed coordinate label on the Moravské Budějovice site was not used. C2RD’s Magnac-Lavalette and Pourrières sites are separate. CIDN’s local airport facilities are grouped, with its forthcoming maritime zone and remote partners excluded. Deelen is separate from Aviolanda. Goyang’s Daedeok airfield is not in Daejeon, and the Anchor Center is not included.

Deferred leads include mobile prototype demonstrations, unresolved campus overlap, regional programmes, unlocated flying areas and planned facilities. The Korean certification and development centres require boundary reconciliation with existing flight-test records. Established KIAST sites and previously recorded university arenas are excluded as overlaps.

All 610 additions include the preserved 590 earlier additions; all 50 originals are also preserved. Automated checks reconcile all 50 decisions with release membership and verify preservation. Planned browser coverage is 665 routes at desktop and mobile sizes, totaling 1,330 journeys. Actual test and production results are recorded in the pull request and GitHub Actions.
