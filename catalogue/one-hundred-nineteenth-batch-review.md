Field locations remain unresolved for several academy mailing addresses. Those candidates are deferred, including Nicotex, BCTech and Airworks, despite evidence of training provision. Courses and classroom subdivisions are not additional locations.

Current operation, access and flight permission are not independently verified. All markers are approximate editorial locality references, not facility coordinates or launch points. Source publication dates are distinct from event dates.
