# Irish island-delivery trial origins

Reviewed 6 November 2026. Two records bring the catalogue to 564 records across 53 countries.

Connemara Airport is explicitly named as the launch site in the university’s September 2019 report. Roonagh Pier is explicitly named as the origin in original July 2018 reporting quoting An Post. Both records retain historical trial status. Source publication dates are not treated as exact flight dates.

Duplicate review covered all 562 existing records and aliases Connemara, Inverin, Indreabhan, Roonagh, Louisburgh, Clare Island, Aran and Diabetes Drone. Each named mainland origin is counted once. Recipient islands, project partners, university premises and pharmacy premises are not added. Map markers represent broad localities, not surveyed launch positions. Current service and access remain unverified.
