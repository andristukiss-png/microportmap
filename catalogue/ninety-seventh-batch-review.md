# Moneygall delivery base

Reviewed 7 November 2026. One record brings the catalogue to 565 records across 53 countries.

Reuters reporting published by RTE on 18 May 2020 documents Manna’s delivery trial in Moneygall. The readable transcript of CTO Alan Hicks’s UXDX EMEA 2022 presentation explicitly identifies Barack Obama Plaza as the drone base and retrospectively describes leaving Moneygall. The record is historical, without an inferred closure date or current availability claim.

Duplicate review covered all 564 existing records and aliases Moneygall, Barack Obama Plaza and Manna. This base is distinct from Oranmore and the Dublin-area entries. Shops, pharmacy, individual flights and recipient homes are not additional records. The map marker is a broad Moneygall locality reference, not a surveyed launch position.
