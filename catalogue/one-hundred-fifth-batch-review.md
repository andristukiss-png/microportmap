# Fifty candidate-site screening tasks

Reviewed 8 November 2026. Completed 50 candidate screenings: 20 accepted records, 28 deferred candidates and two overlapping entries excluded. The catalogue reaches 594 research records across 53 countries, leaving 406 to the target of 1,000.

Each task evaluates one lead for physical aerial testing evidence and distinct site scope. “Completed” means the screening decision is recorded; it does not mean deferred candidates have become verified facilities. The machine-readable ledger is [2026-11-08-fifty-task-review.json](2026-11-08-fifty-task-review.json).

The accepted records describe reported research infrastructure. Current operation, access, bookings and flight permission remain independently unverified. All coordinates are editorial locality references, not surveyed facility positions. Publication dates are retained only when displayed; revision dates, opening years and search-engine age estimates are not substituted for publication dates.

Duplicate review covered the 574 existing names, IDs, operators and site scopes, plus candidate aliases. York YRL is distinguished from the newer ISA building; UTARI is in Fort Worth; ASDF is counted once despite other Hanscom laboratory references. Shared equipment, connected rooms and additional projects are excluded. Michigan M-Air Net and the SDU/HCA Airport lead are not new catalogue sites. The other 28 leads remain out of the catalogue for the specific reasons below.

Retrieval note: York YRL, Ohio State PCVLab and PURL evidence was available through indexed primary-source content; direct page retrieval returned 403/502 errors during review. Their URLs are retained, but this review does not certify source-site availability.

All 524 earlier additions and 50 original records are preserved, with an exact preservation hash in the expansion check. The review ledger is also checked against the release membership. Browser verification covers all 599 routes at desktop and mobile sizes, totaling 1,198 journeys.
