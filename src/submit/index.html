What counts as a microport?

We're looking for projects with a physical drone landing, docking, charging, dispatching, or automated operating facility—not general flying zones or aerial photographs.

  • Use an official announcement, manufacturer case study, or reliable public report.
  • Share a city or project area, not a private dock coordinate.
  • Distinguish plans, trials and operational deployments.
  • Don't submit personal data or sensitive security locations.
