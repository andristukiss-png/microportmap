How to interpret this data

A source URL means a public source is cited. It does not mean the site is operating today, has been independently inspected, or has an accessible public landing address.

Approximate map coordinates are reference points. They are not exact infrastructure footprints, navigational coordinates, or permission to conduct drone operations.

Records are included for research and comparison. Missing fields must remain visibly missing until confirmed by supporting evidence.
